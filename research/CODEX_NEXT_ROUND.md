## Goal

Develop a geometric theory for the **fixed** smooth rational quartic C in P^3 relating:
(a) generic embedded thickenings over k(C);
(b) realization inside each of two integral carrier surfaces;
(c) global realization in P^3; and
(d) compatibility of both carrier embeddings with the same ambient scheme and, when required, equality with their complete intersection.

Initially assume only C is contained in V(F,G)_red, not equality. Study how the exceptional divisor and successive blowups can distinguish a thickening of C from an extra component downstairs. Use valuations and tropical initial ideals as structural languages, not as invitations to enumerate examples.

## Research method

- Start by checking the algebraic definitions and exact relationships among normal cone, projectivized cone, and strict-transform intersection on E.
- Select a concrete theorem or counterexample that clarifies compatibility across levels. Give a conceptual proof, stating every hypothesis.
- Use the multiplicity-two case as a controlled structural test only when it advances the theorem.
- Treat F and G symmetrically. Generalize to arbitrary smooth curves only if the general proof is simpler or materially clarifies the fixed-C problem.
- Distinguish generic formal geometry from specializations along C and global components.
- Do not assume unverified common-singularity claims. Respect the repository's closed quartic-carrier results.
- Do not claim a new theorem merely because computations suggest it; consult existing literature and the repository's provenance records.
