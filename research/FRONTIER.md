# Research frontier — current navigation (October 30, 2026)

## Three principal research programs (not proved implication chains)

These are focused possible next steps, not a mandated order or new theorems. The [synthesis dependency map](synthesis/DEPENDENCIES.md) records the precise gaps.

1. **Global determinant route.** Fix and classify admissible embedded lower thickenings of the quartic; check extension and Gorenstein conditions, then calculate actual homogeneous equation modules. The missing step is a **proper decomposable determinant-zero pair** in the global source, not merely a formal kernel or numerical budget. The October 27 one-step criterion is reviewed source-level work, not a promoted universal existence theorem.
2. **Uniform residual-jet route.** Extend fixed-carrier jet rigidity to a family covering relevant higher-degree carriers, including exceptional rank-drop strata, and prove a uniform residual-degree obstruction for actual proper pairs. One sextic certificate or explicit residual example cannot supply that bound.
3. **Conductor-descent route.** Identify actual higher-degree integral carriers, compute the **full** conductor and inverse-image support, and test descent of genuine hypersurface sections. Necessary normalization models, Cartier class relations, and finite phase reductions alone do not produce mates or exclude every carrier.

**Supporting tools, not independent conclusions:** full initial ideals and valuations (with cancellation and specialization), characteristic-two/zero comparisons (without assuming support-preserving lifting), and intrinsic contact/dual/ruled geometry (without an unproved map to equation modules). The unrestricted characteristic-zero STCI question is open; both hypothetical defining degrees are at least five by the accepted low-degree boundary.

**Research selection rule:** pursue a calculation only when its outcome constrains a family, proves an interface implication, or yields a reusable counterexample/obstruction. Do not restart disconnected case enumeration or treat completed consolidation as an active mathematical problem.

### Failed shortcuts worth retaining as counterexamples or warnings

1. **Primitive e=2 is empty:** false; primitive quadruples exist. A later factorization result excludes specified quartic-mate branches, and R-0001 now excludes all integral quartic mates. Keep the geometric e=2 classification rather than the obsolete exclusion.
2. **Common first normal direction forces a tiny coincidence count:** false without additional sole-support/global hypotheses. An explicit smooth-along-C cubic has six osculating coincidences; a proper pair sharing its saturated direction has residual trisecant lines.
3. **Dependent first symbols determine the whole thickening:** false. The characteristic-two prescribed pair has an essential degree-four initial equation produced by cancellation.
4. **Flat arithmetic lifting preserves reduced support:** false for the mu4 family; characteristic-zero fibers have extra reduced components.
5. **A singleton inverse-support projection admits a mate:** false in R-0017 because nilpotent conductor descent fails.
6. **First-normal, Hilbert-function, or socle budgets alone exclude all STCI pairs:** unsupported; higher contact, saturation defects, and global realization remain.
7. **Exceptional vertical cycles imply downstairs residual curves:** false as a general inference; actual residual cycle or proper intersection argument is needed.
8. **Plane-section length determines local plane algebra:** false; distinct supported rank-four transverse algebras give identical lengths and different embedding dimensions.
10. **A small finite computational PASS proves a global theorem:** false; exact scripts certify stated finite calculations, with inherited classification inputs and untested families explicitly separated.

## General intersection viewpoint (research framing, not a theorem)

For the fixed curve \(C_0\), it is often useful to begin with two surfaces having no common surface component and
\[
C_0\subset V(F,G)_{\mathrm{red}},
\]
without assuming equality. If \(m\) is the generic intersection multiplicity along \(C_0\), the effective fundamental cycle has the form
\[
[V(F,G)]=m[C_0]+R,\qquad de=4m+\deg R,
\]
where \(R\) has no component equal to \(C_0\). The STCI case is precisely \(R=0\). This cycle-level formulation does not identify special-fiber exceptional curves with residual components downstairs: vertical exceptional geometry may instead encode excess contact or degeneration along \(C_0\).

Useful levels of analysis include the generic filtered transverse complete-intersection algebra, its global normal-jet realization, the full associated graded ideal and Rees/successive-blowup geometry, residual intersection cycles, and (when helpful) carrier normalization and conductor descent. These are complementary viewpoints, **not** a required sequence or a ranking of methods. Agents may choose or combine methods based on mathematical leverage, including approaches not listed here. Do not treat the volume of prior results in any lane as evidence that it is intrinsically preferred. In particular, no Hilbert-function strategy, normalization-first approach, or residual-degree bound is mandated.

## Earlier priority: jet-condition and discrepancy framework

**Question.** For primitive or quasiprimitive thickenings
\[
C=C_1\subset C_2\subset\cdots\subset C_m,
\]
when do the successive ambient containment conditions on \(H^0(I_{C_j}(a))\) fail to be independent, and can those dependencies be characterized geometrically?

The earlier quartic order-one \(e=0,1\) calculations remain a laboratory for jet dependencies, **not** surviving quartic STCI-mate cases.

**Leverage.** Uniform order-one bounds, the e=2 primitive-fourth classification, quartic factorization, the ruling/comparison divisor, exact first-symbol ranks, finite local-cohomology reduction, and MF6 constraints.

**First attack.** Express the first few restriction maps intrinsically on the unique quadric containing the rational quartic. Compare expected and actual ranks using the divisor measuring the difference between the primitive conormal direction and the quadric conormal direction.

**Intermediate target.** Prove that rank failure through the first few jets is equivalent to a finite list of special divisor/incidence configurations on the quadric.

## Earlier priority: structural invariants for the entirely-thick branch

**Question.** For exact generic normal orders \(p,q\ge2\), what data beyond the first tangent forms controls the exceptional intersection cycle of the proper transforms of the two defining surfaces?

The local examples show that first-symbol data alone can hide arbitrarily large excess contact.
