# Open interfaces

The characteristic-zero STCI question for the fixed \(C_0\) is open.
Existence and nonexistence remain possible. This page records concrete
mathematical gaps, not an ordered agenda; [selection criteria](RESEARCH_DECISION_GUIDE.md)
help choose a bounded investigation. Exact established statements are in the
[result records](results/INDEX.md) and their proof homes.

The [quadratic conductor refinement](synthesis/quadratic_covers_and_conductor_descent.md#12-embedded-curve-exclusion-of-the-minimal-boundary) gives \(\delta\ge a+b-2\), but no incompatible upper bound. Its Rao dimensions repeat the canonical sequence, while actual multiplication, columns and boundary embedding may supply new information. Finite-order punctual MCM lifts do not produce a homogeneous ideal embedding; vanishing primary complement classes do not produce algebraic sections. An embedded AG conjugate union would descend automatically, but the [known basic-link sources](synthesis/supported_linkage_operations.md) fail generic Gorensteinness or a descended equation test. Other sources and chains remain open.

The [embedded fourth-extension calculation](synthesis/intrinsic_fourth_extension.md) closes the previously missing neighborhood-only route for the two allowed (4,4) types. It does not supply a general classification of higher-length thickenings or higher-degree actual sources; those remain separate interfaces above.

## Tests that distinguish a new implication from a repeated computation

For a proper degree-\((a,b)\) pair containing \(C_0\),
\([V(F,G)]=m[C_0]+R\) and \(ab=4m+\deg R\). The exact finite-length
criterion tests an **actual** pair; a uniform theorem needs quantified source
coverage. Full initial ideals must include cancellation-generated relations.
Exceptional vertical curves do not automatically supply \(R\ne0\).

A leading symbol, an abstract graded Picard relation, an all-orders formal
lift, and a global homogeneous equation are different realizations. In a
fixed symbol fiber, all allowed lifts enter the obstruction quotient; the
[general lifting lemma](synthesis/global_lifting_interfaces.md) makes that
check precise. The conditional sextic seed's exact witness kills the free
\(q^3\) direction, so that seed is an all-lifts exclusion; this says nothing
about every remaining root.

Stopped construction families are not exhausted subjects. Revisit one when
there is a new actual source, a missing boundary calculation, a provable
coverage extension, or an informative counterexample. A new notation or
another equivalent condition alone is not a completed advance.

## Two bounded next investigations

These are alternatives with different payoffs, not an ordered agenda. The first has the clearest existing finite source. The second tests a structural ambiguity. The extremal conductor case is now excluded by the embedded line argument; it is not an open task or a reason to enumerate adjacent lengths without a new mechanism.

1. **Decide the surviving zero-defect two-node sextic pencil at its declared low-order level.** Under the existing conditional (6,16) hypotheses, use the actual pencil \(F_6=H_1H_2+\eta q^3\), with \(\eta\ne0\) necessary for a supported pair. Retain the eleven symbol equations, all twenty-five second-obstruction pairings, full-root torsion including node gluing, and complete cubic source incidence. Use the [rank-certified block map or its proved pivot cover](synthesis/rank_strata_and_sextic_lifts.md#7-a-finite-completion-test-on-each-retained-open); do not cancel the multiplier at its zeros. Stop at coverage-certified emptiness, an exact point satisfying every declared condition with its unique actual sextic, or a precise indispensable missing representation. A survivor still needs a degree-sixteen mate, properness and sole support. This tests the whole surviving stratum, rather than another seed or a closed neighboring chart.

2. **Decide relative defect in a fixed supported length-five degeneration stratum.** Over \(A=k[[t]]\), take \(I=(x,y)\) and regular-sequence ideals \(J\subset A[[x,y]]\) with quotient free of rank five, \(\sqrt J=I\), generic algebra \(k((t))[y]/(y^5)\), and closed algebra \(k[x,y]/(x^2+y^3,xy)\). Can the R-0030 relative comparison defect be nonzero? The [current control](synthesis/comparison_defect_specialization.md) has zero relative defect despite nonzero closed-fiber defect. Use the full relative initial ideal and \(\operatorname{in}_I(IJ)\). Stop at one verified relative counterexample or a coverage theorem for this exact stratum. A failure to parametrize all families is a limitation, not a vanishing proof by samples. This is a local structural question, not a fixed-quartic projective construction.

The surviving universe remains wider than these tests: higher-index and non-Cartier carriers, multiple-branch inverse support, nonsaturated and extra-generator normal patterns, common generic singularities, non-Gorenstein separated branches, and rank-zero matrix transitions are not exhausted. Neither recent success nor an equivalent reformulation makes one program compulsory.
