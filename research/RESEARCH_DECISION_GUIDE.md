# Choosing a bounded investigation

This guide gives selection criteria, not theorem authority or a compulsory
agenda. The [approaches](INDEX.md), [mechanisms](MAP.md), and
[open interfaces](FRONTIER.md) expose the landscape. Choose work for its
mathematical contribution, not the volume of previous files in a lane.

A useful task may construct actual equations, describe a realizable multiple
structure or residual cycle, exclude a covered class,
identify a reusable lemma, make a computation geometric, supply an explicit
counterexample, verify a literature application, or formulate the exact
remaining implication. It need not solve unrestricted STCI to be worthwhile.

An exploratory task may test an **unproved mechanism**. It needs a concrete
input, a meaningful question and an interpretable stopping outcome; it need not
already have the comparison map or construction it is trying to discover.
Failure can identify an essential hypothesis, a useful local model, or the
missing realization condition. Do not require an unrestricted STCI implication
before allowing a bounded geometric question with independent value.

Before expanding a calculation, identify the object and source: abstract curve,
embedded multiple structure, actual global linear system, normalized carrier,
full conductor, surface singularity, or proper projective intersection. State
what either outcome changes and a stopping condition. Check existing proofs
before rederiving a known mechanism.

Before comparing two proposed obstructions, determine whether both follow from the same exact sequence or numerical identity. A repeated budget is not independent evidence. Prefer a task that adds actual source, multiplication, embedding, or coverage information, and state which information it adds. A negative control can retire a detection shortcut without retiring the surrounding subject.

Keep both constructive and obstructive uses visible. A compatibility condition
can suggest a construction. Failure of one family is not nonexistence. An
all-degree theorem about one source is not a theorem about all sources; source
dimension or density does not prove paired support. A stopped target is not an
exhausted subject.

Characteristic is substantive. The fixed quartic has a quartic-carrier STCI
in every positive characteristic, whereas characteristic-zero quartic carriers
are excluded. For a transfer, identify the precise use of separability,
invertible integers, Frobenius, duality, or a global surface bound. An algebraic
formula can extend even when the argument using it does not.

Use positive-characteristic solutions to check the claimed scope of characteristic-free necessary conditions. They do not prescribe how a characteristic-zero construction must look. The [frontier's candidate investigations](FRONTIER.md#concrete-candidates) are alternatives, not a replacement for the broader approach landscape. Compare the elliptic support construction, sextic cyclic-cover question, downstairs ACM target, conditional sextic incidence and relative-defect question on their actual inputs and possible mathematical payoffs.

Separate a currently running assignment from a candidate or an open question
without a workable entry point. A framing change does not redirect an active
investigation by itself. A previous decision that no experiment was selected
applies to the inputs considered then; it does not prohibit later proposals.
Revisit a closed family only when the new question changes an actual hypothesis,
source, boundary calculation or coverage claim.

For a claim, retrieve its exact statement and proof, then its necessary
computation. Reuse review of unchanged inputs. Independently review material
new deductions, changed proofs and consequential preservation decisions.
Retain useful mathematics and compact support, not a new administrative
hierarchy. Follow [authority](AUTHORITY.md) and [contribution](CONTRIBUTING_AGENT.md)
rules for status and promotion.
