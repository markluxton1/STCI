# Choosing a bounded investigation

This guide gives selection criteria, not theorem authority or a compulsory
agenda. The [approaches](INDEX.md), [mechanisms](MAP.md), and
[open interfaces](FRONTIER.md) expose the landscape. Choose work for its
mathematical contribution, not the volume of previous files in a lane.

A useful task may construct actual equations, describe a realizable multiple
structure or residual cycle, exclude a covered class,
identify a reusable lemma, make a computation geometric, supply an explicit
counterexample, verify a literature application, or formulate the exact
remaining implication. It need not solve unrestricted STCI to be worthwhile.

An exploratory task may test an **unproved mechanism**. It needs a concrete
input, a meaningful question and an interpretable stopping outcome; it need not
already have the comparison map or construction it is trying to discover.
Failure can identify an essential hypothesis, a useful local model, or the
missing realization condition. Do not require an unrestricted STCI implication
before allowing a bounded geometric question with independent value. For example,
the [spectral determinant construction](synthesis/spectral_determinant_schemes.md)
produces graded complete-intersection families while leaving ambient realization
open; the [global jet filtration](synthesis/global_equation_jets.md) identifies
which source conditions an actual construction must satisfy.

Before expanding a calculation, identify the object and source: abstract curve,
embedded multiple structure, actual global linear system, normalized carrier,
full conductor, surface singularity, or proper projective intersection. State
what either outcome changes and a stopping condition. Check existing proofs
before rederiving a known mechanism.

Before comparing two proposed obstructions, determine whether both follow from the same exact sequence or numerical identity. A repeated budget is not independent evidence. Prefer a task that adds actual source, multiplication, embedding, or coverage information, and state which information it adds. A negative control can retire a detection shortcut without retiring the surrounding subject. The [pencil Milnor comparison](synthesis/pencil_milnor_budget.md) is a concrete example: its quartic inequality follows from stronger local geometry, while the general identity and realization questions remain useful.

Keep both constructive and obstructive uses visible. A compatibility condition
can suggest a construction. Failure of one family is not nonexistence. An
all-degree theorem about one source is not a theorem about all sources; source
dimension or density does not prove paired support. A stopped target is not an
exhausted subject.

Characteristic is substantive. The fixed quartic has a quartic-carrier STCI
in every positive characteristic, whereas characteristic-zero quartic carriers
are excluded. For a transfer, identify the precise use of separability,
invertible integers, Frobenius, duality, or a global surface bound. An algebraic
formula can extend even when the argument using it does not.

Use positive-characteristic solutions to check the claimed scope of characteristic-free necessary conditions. They do not prescribe how a characteristic-zero construction must look. The [frontier's recommended tests](FRONTIER.md#recommended-next-bounded-tests) are alternatives, not a replacement for the broader approach landscape. Compare the elliptic support construction, sextic cyclic-cover question, downstairs ACM target, conditional sextic incidence and relative-defect question on their actual inputs and possible mathematical payoffs.

Separate a currently running assignment from a candidate or an open question
without a workable entry point. A framing change does not redirect an active
investigation by itself. A previous decision that no experiment was selected
applies to the inputs considered then; it does not prohibit later proposals.
Revisit a closed family only when the new question changes an actual hypothesis,
source, boundary calculation or coverage claim.

For a claim, retrieve its exact statement and proof, then its necessary
computation. Reuse review of unchanged inputs. Independently review material
new deductions, changed proofs and consequential preservation decisions.
Retain useful mathematics and compact support, not a new administrative
hierarchy. Follow [authority](AUTHORITY.md) and [contribution](CONTRIBUTING_AGENT.md)
rules for scoped proof review and evidence.


## What the combined work changes

The strongest shared lesson is that actual source images and full support
carry information absent from an abstract normal form, a graded algebra or
a scalar budget. The [spectral construction](synthesis/spectral_determinant_schemes.md)
produces graded families, and [order-three roots](synthesis/order_three_roots_and_sextic_lifts.md)
produce positive first embedded lifts. Their actual sextic source conditions
still fail on the entire tested affine slice. Conversely, the
[actual octic calculation](synthesis/actual_octic_root_lift_comparison.md)
constructs a proper pair with positive residual degree. These outcomes identify
where compatibility can hold and where the next condition enters; they are
not evidence votes for an unrestricted answer.

Three distinctions now make the next work more selective:

- **Root existence versus equation membership.** The intrinsic sextic map
  tests every allowed first equation correction. On exact-order-three roots,
  later sextic membership is automatic if the compatible root lifts exist.
  Repeated membership calculations would miss the remaining root-existence,
  mate, properness and support obligations.
- **Support versus descent on an actual carrier.** The toric and elliptic
  sources have actual sections and polarizations. Full inverse support is a
  concrete first test; only its survivors need conductor or power descent.
  Local gluing controls retain independent geometric value but do not bypass
  those projective conditions.
- **New information versus another form of the same constraint.** The
  Milnor inequality is locally automatic for C0. The prescribed Chow bundles
  already encode equations. The successful quintic ADE argument adds the
  location of the embedded defect and controlled cover geometry to a rank
  budget. Future inequalities need similarly identifiable extra information.

Positive characteristic is part of this account. Quartic-carrier STCIs exist
for every p>0, so a characteristic-free obstruction must fail on those actual
examples or have an unmet hypothesis. Frobenius powers, trace, separability
and division by the characteristic are mathematical steps to inspect, not
labels to append. The arithmetic lane has an explicit required endpoint root
defect in prescribed supported lifts; no theorem yet identifies it with a
spectral Picard class, a carrier Weil class or the surface-normalization
conductor. A new comparison must construct its map and preserve its hypotheses.

When using the [prescribed quartic family](results/quartic_mu4_degeneration.md)
as a specialization control, track both the residual curves of the generic
fiber and the new order-four relation in the special fiber's normal initial
ideal. An argument that requires base change for the normal graded pieces
must check it separately from flatness of the projective family. The
[integral chart calculation](results/quartic_normal_initial_base_change.md)
exhibits the precise failure to test for.

## Torus symmetry as an organizing method

Use the [supported-boundary framework](frameworks/torus_supported_boundaries.md)
when a declared moving family has accessible full ideals and equation maps.
Use the [embedded-quotient framework](frameworks/embedded_torus_quotients.md)
for specified finite images, endpoint descent, and source-space tests. The
reusable [purity lemma](results/pure_curve_degree_exhaustion.md) explains the
extra hypothesis that turns inclusion plus degree exhaustion into equality;
the [generator criterion](results/moving_ideal_generator_criterion.md) explains
why two independent sections are insufficient. These independently reviewed
inputs support bounded investigations and require their literal hypotheses.

Choose a next test by what it can change: an actual supported two-generator
point gives a construction; emptiness on a declared family limits that family;
a specialization map can connect two previously separate data sets. Repeating
an eigenpair exclusion or a numerical endpoint budget supplies no all-family
coverage. The pilot's
[Investigation records](investigations/README.md) state stopping points and
remaining dependencies explicitly.

**Necessary invariant-limit principle.** If a degree-(a,b) complete
intersection has reduced support exactly C0, then the full flat limit of its
ideal under the one-parameter torus action is a T-invariant scheme with
reduced support exactly C0 and the same complete-intersection Hilbert
polynomial. Indeed, for some N its ideal lies between I_C0^N and I_C0;
these torus-stable containments persist in the full flat limit, so the
support cannot acquire residual curves or disappear. This is a necessary
condition for *every* hypothetical supported STCI, not merely for
T-invariant defining equations.

The [full flat-limit account](synthesis/global_equations_and_constructions.md#the-supported-hilbert-locus-and-its-full-torus-boundary)
also explains the essential qualification: the invariant limit need not
remain a complete intersection, or even admit two generators. Therefore
excluding invariant **pairs of equations** with sole support is not enough.
A potentially sufficient obstruction would exclude *all* T-invariant
sole-supported ideals of the required Hilbert polynomial that arise as
full flat limits of degree-(a,b) complete intersections. Equivalently,
one can investigate the intersection of the torus-fixed Hilbert locus
with the closure of the supported complete-intersection locus, retaining
the marked support condition in the degeneration. A fixed-locus
classification alone is not an obstruction without the deformation or
coverage step.

The proposed direction is to study invariant boundary ideals together
with deformations preserving support that can recover CI or ACM structure.
The general member must be allowed to break symmetry. Limiting two
individual forms discards relations and can leave a common surface factor.

For a specified finite embedded quotient, the
[endpoint-descent criterion](synthesis/toric_quotients_and_endpoint_descent.md)
recovers its actual homogeneous source spaces by finite weightwise linear
algebra. A proper pair in the prescribed degrees, together with degree
exhaustion, decides that image. The length-four comparison shows why the
embedding and the full infinity algebra matter: the same nilpotent quotient
type yields different postulation in characteristic zero, two and three.
This is a useful bounded realization task, not a reduction of arbitrary
characteristic-zero pairs to a constant quotient.

The [explicit sextic configurations](synthesis/toric_sextic_residuals_and_limits.md)
now supply exact controls for splitting, absorption and loss of CI generators.
The sharp quotient-length bound is eight for proper eigenform sextics in every
characteristic. A length-nine sole-support search must therefore change the
eigenform hypotheses; the existing all-pair conormal reduction still needs
moving embedded data and actual source lifts. Recomputing the 23-dimensional
double source in quotient coordinates adds an interpretation, not a second
obstruction.

This can organize either a fixed-degree CI question such as (6,6), or the
independent downstairs ACM objective. Its value is a structured boundary and
weight decomposition; no finite fixed-locus classification, tractable
supported deformation space or general recovery theorem is currently supplied.
The explicit ACM three-generator limit has the (6,6) Hilbert series but retains
boundary curves; it is not a sole-support boundary ideal.
Before assigning
a substantial calculation, select an actual invariant ideal, check its target
Hilbert polynomial and support incidence, and identify the full deformation
and generator conditions. Existing [invariant quadric multiples](synthesis/section_defects_and_exceptional_cover_controls.md#gorenstein-multiples-still-need-higher-twist-information)
are actual non-ACM controls, not demonstrated limits of the desired CI type.
Their failure to be ACM does not alone exclude an ACM general fiber.
Retain embedded points if present; a pure hull changes the boundary object.

A successful supported deformation constructs a new ideal even if it is not
an inverse of a torus-orbit degeneration. An obstruction at one chosen boundary
ideal excludes only the declared deformation family. A negative reduction of
all degree-(a,b) pairs needs coverage of their possible invariant limits and
their deformations; a fixed power neighborhood must also have that coverage.
Thus this is a proposed organizing method, not a replacement for already
available actual equation spaces or a completed reduction to invariant CIs.

## Recommended allocation and stopping outcomes

For complementary bounded work, prefer **octic multiplier geometry** for
positive containing intersections and **higher contact in the three ADE
(6,6) triple orbits** for a covered degree sector. This is a strategy judgment,
not an assignment, an assertion of computational ease or a prediction about
unrestricted STCI. The torus method can sharpen either the (6,6) or ACM target
once a useful boundary ideal and deformation representation are specified.
The [frontier](FRONTIER.md#recommended-next-bounded-tests) gives exact tests and
stopping outcomes.

| Objective | Best available entry and next information | Scope or readiness limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Construct containing intersections | Exact octic multiplier kernels and fixed divisors at known first-compatible roots; joint generation activates the existing proper-pair construction | The generic construction already gives length eight and residual degree 32; higher contact is a new condition, and sole support needs length sixteen |
| Decide a covered part of (6,6) | Full higher-contact incidence in all three surviving ADE primitive-triple orbits, retaining the actual 27-dimensional sources | A sector theorem requires the ADE general-pencil hypothesis and complete orbit coverage; the unrestricted sextic singularity/cover compatibility remains open |
| Decide descent for an embedded finite quotient family | Full endpoint image lattices and actual degree spaces from the constant-quotient criterion; test properness and image base change | Exact for the specified image. Extending to a hypothetical characteristic-zero sextic CI needs a moving rank-nine family, not only collisions of abstract quotient points |
| Organize supported CI/ACM deformations by symmetry | Full opposite-limit attracting strata on a bounded supported Hilbert locus, or a specified invariant boundary ideal with verified full relations and supported deformations | Test two-generation of moving ideals; fixed eigenform pairs and tangent directions alone do not decide it. Any exhaustive claim requires coverage |
| Construct a homogeneous ACM thickening downstairs | An actual ideal or new projection source that changes the known missing-section mechanism; allow more than two generators | Independent value even without AG/CI; ACM upstairs does not imply ACM after projection |
| Resolve the conditional order-three equation family | The intrinsic sextic map over a justified cover or component of the full fixed-frame root locus, retaining all rank strata and corrections | This belongs to the conditional (6,16) bridge, not the (6,6) reduction; later root lifts, mate and support remain obligations |
| Finish a constructed carrier family | Remaining toric M incidence with a resource bound, or the elliptic family's fiberwise full-inverse-support test | A negative result closes the covered source; a support-positive survivor still needs actual descent |
| Learn which obstruction depends on the embedding | Transport one specified marked structure and its corrected equation map along smooth projection centers leaving the C0 orbit | Exploratory: the relative source is missing. The existing center moduli are not such a family, and changing C0 is distinct from torus translation |

The toric M locus remains a good closeout choice when finishing that actual
carrier class is the objective. Its small remaining description alone is not
a reason to rank it above a positive construction or a covered degree sector.
The normal sextic cyclic cover offers a broader structural question: compare
its required index-nine local class and embedded defects with actual global
singularity and polarization data. Recomputing its known invariants, or
treating the normal cover as a smooth resolution, supplies no new restriction.

The arithmetic next test should seek an actual algebraic compatibility
condition on its endpoint defect beyond the genus budget. More finite
precision by powering, or an unrelated normal-form analogy, does not supply
that condition. A fixed small-characteristic experiment becomes useful after
its relation to this concrete defect is stated.

The consolidation lane's contribution is to keep these interfaces navigable:
one full owner per reusable proof, exact computational consumers, and explicit
proved or missing arrows. A useful new contribution changes a source, a
compatibility map, a coverage statement or a constructive outcome. It should
update the owning proof and the relevant frontier together; another chronological
assessment is not needed merely because a branch has ended.

## Review standard: strongly consider invariant-limit stratification

For each fixed degree pair, strongly consider using full T-invariant flat limits to organize the bounded supported Hilbert locus into attracting sets, and, where useful, test **two-generation of moving ideals on each set**. The fixed ideal need not be a complete intersection. Distinguish the full-ideal limit, eigenform pairs, and I-adic initial ideals. Analyze graded minimal generators and the actual homogeneous equation incidence, including relations and scheme-theoretic equality. Tangent weights and smoothing of selected fixed points are auxiliary tools rather than the primary classification. Any claim of exhaustive strata must prove coverage and handle the chosen bound N; do not infer unrestricted exclusion from a bounded calculation.

This replaces selecting a single invariant boundary ideal as the default first step, without requiring exhaustive stratification before local geometric work. Existing degree-sector, quotient, carrier and positive-characteristic projects remain useful as sources of geometric strata, controls, and equations; no theorem status changes.
