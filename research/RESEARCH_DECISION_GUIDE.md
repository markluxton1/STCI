# What matters now: STCI research decision guide

This is a **navigation and prioritization guide**, not a new theorem, proof audit, or assignment of an exclusive active frontier. Consult [STATE.md](STATE.md) and the [canonical results](results/INDEX.md) for authoritative statements. The unrestricted fixed rational quartic STCI question remains open.

The current selection rule is the user-directed [STCI equation search](STCI_SEARCH.md): generate a plausible actual source, test it exactly, diagnose failure, and revise the source. Prefer work that either constructs equations, rules out an explicitly quantified source, or identifies its next unresolved condition. Compare positive and negative evidence. Large parameter spaces, sophisticated local data and positive residual bounds do not independently answer the search question. The characteristic-p lane must distinguish fixed-carrier bounds from an all-pairs minimum; the freedom lane must retain actual global source images and common factors.

The [October 29 continuation](notes/2026-10-29-search-continuation.md) supplies two completed scoped endpoints and one partial computation. The mixed-weight seed is exhausted at mate degree six, so repeating its generic sextic samples adds no evidence. The arithmetic jet-gap source has an attained growing minimum, but extending that mechanism to arbitrary pairs is a separate problem. The full marked-quadric admissible/descent locus remains a bounded unresolved incidence; its computed empty slices do not settle it.

The October 27–28 construction agenda below is retained as a set of available inputs, not a competing current assignment.

The [October 27 research vision](notes/2026-10-27-containing-intersections-research-vision.md) adopts a constructive, residual-inclusive framing: understand actual proper intersections containing \(C\), then ask how to reach particular thickness and residual configurations. New reviewed deductions there remain provisional. Changes of residual geometry at fixed generic multiplicity and transfer of residual mass onto \(C\) are distinct experiments; acquiring a common surface factor leaves the proper-pair problem. Compare both positive and negative evidence, including odd-characteristic constructions and global formal-section extension, without assuming the characteristic-zero answer.

The [arbitrary-degree continuation](notes/2026-10-27-arbitrary-degree-absorption-synthesis.md) makes the selection rule explicit: formulate a general construction, criterion or competing assertion first. Before substantial degree-specific work, identify the principle being tested, what each possible outcome would teach, and which argument survives a change of degrees. An empty \((6,6)\) endpoint would settle only that degree pair; there is no reduction of arbitrary equations to sextics. Positive constructions and exact limits of known methods are substantive outcomes even when they do not decide STCI.

The [October 28 construction picture](notes/2026-10-28-containing-intersections-synthesis.md) sharpens this criterion: a useful residual deformation must retain the actual low equations and the fixed marked partner. A once-linked curve can remain flat through absorption while full-primary extraction jumps. Every attachment divisor is constructible in a sufficient range, so concentrating attachment alone is not evidence of increasing thickness. The established Fano model supplies alternative actual section spaces, and prescribed-index carriers show that local singularity data and global divisor relations are separate construction tasks. These reviewed proposals motivate an integrated family experiment; they do not make one carrier model or degree pair compulsory. Check the [focused primary literature](notes/2026-10-28-containing-intersections-literature.md) before a new bridge, especially the distinction between Picard, Weil and almost-Cartier data.

The [residual-degree bounds](notes/2026-10-28-residual-degree-synthesis.md) provide quantitative selection criteria. Specify which independent input is controlled: marked attachment, numerical self-intersection and its gap, exact normal orders/root slopes, or full-attachment cohomology. Compute the resulting floor and its equality mechanism before choosing an example. A zero bound identifies missing geometry; it does not prove an endpoint exists. A positive bound on a specified carrier or root configuration does not cover arbitrary pairs. The Fano graph supplies no automatic additional nef direction, so any stronger use of its conductor must identify extra geometric input explicitly.

## Research selection principles (editorial guidance, not theorem)

Choose work by the chance of obtaining a global, independently checkable mathematical implication, clarity of hypotheses, and the size of the genuinely surviving class addressed. Prefer comparing complementary approaches over extending a computation simply because it is already underway. Current activity is described in [FRONTIER.md](FRONTIER.md); this guide should not designate a single active assignment. A change of research interest is not evidence that an earlier direction failed.

## Research viewpoints and agent discretion (editorial guidance, not theorem)

The broader question can start from \(C_0\subset V(F,G)_{\mathrm{red}}\) with no common surface component, without imposing STCI in advance. Write \([V(F,G)]=m[C_0]+R\) and \(de=4m+\deg R\). An STCI has \(R=0\); conversely, forcing \(R\ne0\) excludes an STCI presentation for the chosen pair. Exceptional vertical curves do not automatically imply residual components downstairs.

Possible complementary viewpoints are: (a) generic filtered transverse algebras and full initial ideals; (b) global realization by normal jets and homogeneous equations; (c) Rees algebras, successive blowups, exceptional and residual intersection cycles; (d) carrier geometry, normalization, and conductor descent. None is a required first step. Work on one level can be valuable even before it forces a residual component.

The existing frontier offers several **nonexclusive** entry points: [jet dependence/discrepancy](FRONTIER.md), [entirely-thick successive transforms](results/entirely_thick.md), [root and formal-lifting questions](results/INDEX.md), and [fixed-carrier projection/conductor questions](questions/cartier_projection_models.md). Agents should choose or combine them based on potential mathematical leverage, not the number of existing records, convenience of case-splitting, or a mandated strategy. The possible Hilbert-function approach is one hypothesis to evaluate, not an adopted priority. New ideas outside these lanes are welcome if their assumptions and relationship to the original problem are explicit.

## What to ignore by default

- Historical handoffs and dated state snapshots when determining *current* theorem status.
- Legacy quartic B1/B2/B3/D mate cases as open cases: [STCI-R-0001](results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) closed them for the fixed curve.
- Individual validation logs or scratch outputs unless a canonical result, a reproducibility question, or an active computation points to them.
- New synthesis/audit notes as proof authority. They can clarify interfaces but cannot promote results.
- Generalization notes unrelated to the actual question. When characteristic comparison is requested, include positive-characteristic constructions with their literal hypotheses; no theorem transfer to characteristic zero is presumed.

## How to find evidence efficiently

**Question -> canonical record -> exact cited proof/acceptance -> independent audit -> certificate/computation (only if necessary).**

1. Find the ID in [results/INDEX.md](results/INDEX.md) or the tracked [question STCI-Q-0001](questions/cartier_projection_models.md).
2. Read the result's YAML scope, hypotheses, evidence, audits, and limitations *before* the exposition.
3. Follow the evidence links in that record; many point to preserved historical notes. Consult [source-note inventory](archive/ULTRA_SOURCE_NOTES_INDEX.md) only when the record lacks a direct path.
4. For exact computations use the [certificate map](certificates/README.md), [computation guide](computations/README.md), and [validation inventory](archive/ULTRA_VALIDATION_RECURSIVE_INVENTORY.md). Do not infer a certificate from an output filename.
5. If a note conflicts with a canonical result, apply [AUTHORITY.md](AUTHORITY.md); record the discrepancy rather than silently broadening the theorem.

## Completion test for future work

A new research contribution should answer at least one of: **(i)** exclude a genuinely surviving class of actual STCI presentations; **(ii)** establish a new general geometric statement with explicit hypotheses and proof, including existence/flexibility results; **(iii)** compute a previously unresolved necessary/sufficient test for an actual candidate family; **(iv)** repair a substantive gap in an accepted proof; **(v)** construct or analyze an actual family of intersections containing \(C\), retaining its residual schemes and global realization conditions, in a way that changes the next constructive test. A restatement, inventory, or new notation alone does not meet this bar.

This guide ranks relevance and directs reading; it does not claim exhaustive dependency tracing across every historical file.
