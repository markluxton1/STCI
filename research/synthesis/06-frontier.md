# 6. Surviving mathematical frontier

**Unrestricted question:** does any characteristic-zero pair of homogeneous equations cut out the fixed \(C_0\) set-theoretically? Still open.

The principal directions are distinct:

1. **Simultaneous equations and blowups:** understand common first-normal factors, their exceptional curves, special-fiber degeneration, and excess intersection under successive transforms. Recent chat investigations are provisional until their proofs and scope are independently audited.
2. **Jets and thickenings:** explain geometrically when ambient jet-containment conditions fail to be independent, including entirely-thick and order-one branches beyond the excluded quartic carriers.
3. **Actual versus graded roots:** extend or replace the restrictive [STCI-R-0006](../results/actual_ci_smooth_root_bridge.md) bridge; address singular/nonreduced roots, saturation defects, and exceptional initial-ideal patterns.
4. **Finite-stage obstruction computation:** determine the full quadratic-root second-zero locus and spectral torsion intersection in [STCI-R-0007](../results/quadratic_root_obstruction_model.md).
5. **Higher-degree carriers:** calculate admissible projections and full-conductor descent for the [Cartier singleton models](../questions/cartier_projection_models.md), while remembering that other normalization patterns remain outside that classification.

See [FRONTIER.md](../FRONTIER.md) for the detailed working priorities. A finite reduction is not a completed exclusion; a necessary model is not an example; a special-family obstruction is not an unrestricted theorem.
