# A supported length-five family whose comparison defect does not specialize

The statement and full calculation are in
[R-0064](../results/supported_length_five_defect_specialization.md).
The family has a finite free intersection algebra with sole reduced support
on the marked line. The relative comparison is an isomorphism, while the
actual closed transverse slice has a defect in normal degree four. The
[normal-flat specialization theorem](../results/normal_flat_comparison_specialization.md)
gives the hypotheses under which the two defects agree after specialization.

## Independent review

Independent reconstruction checked the finite free actual algebra, all powers of its normal ideal, the full initial ideal, terminating filtered division for the equation module, and the comparison in every degree. All identities are integral and require no characteristic exclusions. The closed-fiber mismatch and the doubled residual conic in the intersection of the two ordinarily homogenized cubics were checked separately. See the [open sole-support question](../FRONTIER.md#punctual-relative-defect-under-sole-support).
