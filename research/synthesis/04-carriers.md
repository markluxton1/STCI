# 4. Carriers, normalization, and conductor

This lane fixes one integral carrier \(X\) and asks whether some second equation cuts out only \(C_0\) on \(X\). The finite normalization \(\nu:S\to X\) replaces the ambient equation problem by divisor-class relations **plus descent across the conductor**. Support, including isolated inverse-image points and nonreduced conductor defects, matters.

[STCI-R-0003](../results/fixed_carrier_conductor_criterion.md) gives an exact **fixed-carrier** full-conductor criterion. It does not compute the needed lattice for all possible carriers.

Under the additional assumption that the full reduced inverse image is a **single Cartier curve mapping isomorphically** to \(C_0\), [STCI-R-0002](../results/cartier_singleton_lift.md) restricts the normalization/polarization to minimal-degree models; the remaining fixed-curve possibilities include degree-eight and degree-sixteen carrier models. These are **necessary models, not constructions**.

[STCI-R-0004](../results/projection_phase_reduction.md) turns mate existence in fixed admissible projection families into a torsion-coset question involving full-conductor phase data. The actual admissible images and torsion intersections are not yet computed. See [STCI-Q-0001](../questions/cartier_projection_models.md).

This carrier approach and the simultaneous blowup/normal-form approach are complementary: the former studies global divisor descent on one surface, while the latter tracks interaction of both defining equations. No general equivalence between their proposed obstructions has been established.
