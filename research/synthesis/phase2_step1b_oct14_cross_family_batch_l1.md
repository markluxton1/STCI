## Distinct contributions

1. **Normal-carrier index and socle.** For actual local Cartier index r dividing mate multiplicity n, k=n/r, the source proves D_n=(k-1)A_F+kD_r using full embedded ideals, Jacobian socle and local normality. Its global exclusion inherits other premises; do not transfer it to arbitrary nonnormal carriers or unverified P-010 reductions.
2. **Normal carrier class torsion.** For fixed C0, h=gcd(a,4), Delta=(a/h)C-(4/h)H in Cl(X), a mate exists on fixed normal X iff Delta is torsion. Minimum mate degree is 4 ord(Delta)/h. Low-degree exclusions force nonzero torsion. Torsion-free Pic of a resolution does not imply torsion-free Cl(X) without saturated exceptional lattice.
3. **Nonendpoint quintic gcd-three.** Acceptance covers zero-cubic and proportional families, lambda-one boundary, and 3+2 original-six-generator witness. Exact scope: characteristic-zero A4 genus-one normalization, delta nonzero, admissible projection. Does not settle other gcd or normalization strata.
4. **Projection conductor contact phase.** Residual unit torsion corresponds to equality of contact divisors on normalized geometric pair correspondences plus root-of-unity constants. Nilpotent conductor obstruction remains separate; the source does not bound root-of-unity orders.
5. **Cartier singleton lift classification.** Under mate, single smooth rational inverse curve and Cartierness upstairs, normalization is minimal-degree P2, scroll or rational-normal cone; smoothness of normalization is not required away from the lifted curve. Actual projection and conductor descent remain open.
6. **Moving pure-power tower.** Obstruction bundles Q_k for a saturated moving first relation; cohomological vanishing constructs finite-order thickenings, not projective complete intersections. Higher relation and saturation defects cannot be silently discarded.
7. **Symbolic ribbon independent review.** Symbolic I_i/I_(i+1) line bundles and Euler formula under actual Cartier multiple; generically nonzero conormal first-layer map needed for stronger bound. Not ordinary ideal square.
8. **D4 cyclic class-cover counterexample.** Smooth curve with class order 2 whose quasi-etale normalized cyclic cover has two unramified inverse branches, not one ramified branch. This defeats an overgeneralized local ramification heuristic, not global STCI.
