# 5. What has been excluded

The principal completed result is [STCI-R-0001](../results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md): for the fixed monomial rational quartic over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, **no integral degree-four hypersurface containing the curve has a set-theoretic mate of any positive degree**.

The assembled proof combines normal and nonnormal quartic analyses, including normalized plane-sectional genus cases and conductor corrections. Its detailed dependency assembly and independent audit are linked from the canonical theorem; this chapter is a map, not a replacement proof.

Earlier exclusions of plane, quadric, and cubic carriers, together with the audited factor reduction, yield the necessary condition that both degrees in a hypothetical presentation are at least five. See [current state](../STATE.md) and the earlier proofs in [research record](../RESEARCH_RECORD.md). Do not infer that the degree-five or higher cases have been settled.

Historical quartic calculations—order-one, primitive e=2, normal-carrier cases, MF6, and local-cohomology ancestors—should be read in two ways: their original partial proofs remain valuable, but they are **not independent surviving quartic-mate possibilities** after STCI-R-0001. Some ancestor-incidence questions remain mathematically open for reasons separate from quartic-mate existence.
