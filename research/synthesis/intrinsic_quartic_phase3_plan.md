Build a geometry-first dossier for C0 = [s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4] in P3. Preserve intrinsic facts, exact incidence formulas, counterexamples, open questions, and independently checked provisional calculations. Separate geometric statements from applications to set-theoretic complete intersections.

1. Quadric and parametrization: unique quadric, (1,3) class, automorphisms and involutions. Canonical anchors R-0026 through R-0029 as appropriate; check the canonical index for exact assignment.
2. Osculation and special lines: hyperosculating points, osculating planes, paired secants, bitangent correspondence, intersection cycles and degenerations.
3. Dual geometry: degree-six dual, singular osculating and bitangent loci, dual intersections and invariant identities; preserve scheme-theoretic distinctions.
4. Ruled cubic and normalization: intrinsic incidence, double-line geometry, F1 scroll and P4 lift. Cross-reference Phase 2 conductor analysis rather than duplicating its descent proofs.
5. P4 viewpoint: rational normal quartic, projection center, resolved projection, flag and Delta constructions, cubic scrolls, other ruled models, and precise finite-versus-birational morphism distinctions.
6. Computational and arithmetic examples: exact scripts, characteristic-two changes, exceptional root collisions, and explicit counterexamples with the weakest supported scope.
