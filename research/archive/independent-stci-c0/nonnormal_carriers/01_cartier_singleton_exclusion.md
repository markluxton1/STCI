# [RETRACTED / GAP IDENTIFIED] Exclusion of Cartier Singleton Non-Normal Carriers

> [!CAUTION]
> **GAP IDENTIFIED & STATUS UPDATE:**
> The deduction in Step 6 claiming that $\Delta \cdot c = 20$ (or 51) forces at least 15 (or 45) distinct ordinary double points off $c$ is **unestablished**.
> $\Delta \cdot c$ is an intersection multiplicity, not a count of distinct points. High contact multiplicity or non-reduced conductor singularities can absorb this intersection number without requiring 20 distinct points. Furthermore, immersion at a conductor point does not by itself prove that the normalization fiber contains a second distinct point.
