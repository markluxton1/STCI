# Primitive e=2 quartic results

Canonical status: **PROVED for the fixed monomial quartic in the stated \((4,7)\) and \((4,8)\) branches**

For
\[
C_0=[s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4]
\]
and first quotient \(L=O(-5)\), a basepoint-free quotient \(O(-7)^2\twoheadrightarrow O(-5)\) is represented by two binary quadratics \(A,B\) with nonzero homogeneous resultant \(\Delta\).

The universal fourth-obstruction calculation shows that, on \(\Delta\ne0\), its reduced zero locus is exactly
\[
b_0=-2a_1,\qquad b_1=-2a_2.
\]
Thus primitive quadruples of this type do exist; the older blanket primitive-quadruple exclusion is false.

On the entire fourth-obstruction zero locus, the canonical primitive triple has a distinguished cubic \(T\) and the audited ambient-contact calculation gives
\[
H^0(I_{C_3}(4))=T\,H^0(O_{\mathbf P^3}(1)).
\]
Hence every quartic containing the canonical triple factors. This excludes the \(e=2\) branches of hypothetical \((4,7)\) and \((4,8)\) STCI presentations.

It does **not** prove an exclusion for arbitrary \((4,b)\) with larger \(b\), nor for \(e=0,1\).

The dx=1/P-044 residual slice is independently closed by the later saturation certificate; on its basepoint-free locus P-044 is exactly P-045.
