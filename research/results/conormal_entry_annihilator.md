# The conormal entry ideal annihilates the filtration defect

## Scope

Let \(C\hookrightarrow P\) be a regular codimension-two embedding of locally
Noetherian schemes, and let \(J\subset I_C\) be any coherent ideal. Work locally
with ambient ring \(R\), ideal \(I=(x,y)\) generated by a regular sequence,
and \(B=R/I\). The assertion holds in arbitrary characteristic and for marked
components of intersections with residual support. For the description by
carrier singularities, take \(C\) smooth in a smooth threefold and the carriers
to be Cartier hypersurfaces containing \(C\).

## Statement

Set \(E=I/I^2\), \(D=J/IJ\), and \(S=\operatorname{gr}_I R=\operatorname{Sym}_B E\).
Let \(H=\operatorname{in}_I(J)\) and \(T=\operatorname{in}_I(IJ)\) be the
full initial ideals, with \(S_+\) the positive-degree ideal. The
[R-0030 comparison](local_filtration_comparison_defect.md) has kernel
\(\Delta=T/S_+H\) inside \(H/S_+H\). Let \(\phi:D\to E\) be the first
conormal map. The ideal
\[
\mathfrak a=\operatorname{im}\bigl(E^\vee\otimes_B D
  \xrightarrow{1\otimes\phi} E^\vee\otimes_B E
  \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ev}}B\bigr)
  =\operatorname{Fitt}_1\bigl(I/(I^2+J)\bigr)
\]
is generated locally by the entries of \(\phi\), using a basis of \(E\)
and any finite generating set of \(D\). It satisfies
\[
\mathfrak a\Delta=0.
\]
The defect is therefore canonically a module on the closed subscheme
defined by \(\mathfrak a\) in \(C\).

For \(J=I_X+I_Y\) with two Cartier carriers \(X,Y\), keep the labelled
source lines \(I_X/I_C I_X\) and \(I_Y/I_C I_Y\) and their maps into \(E\).
The ideal \(\mathfrak a\) is the sum of the entry ideals of these two maps
and depends only on \(J\subset I\). In the smooth setting its zero set is
\(C\cap\operatorname{Sing}X\cap\operatorname{Sing}Y\).

At a DVR stalk with uniformizer \(t\) and \(\mathfrak a=(t^r)\ne0\),
every elementary divisor of \(\Delta_n\) has exponent at most \(r\), and
\[
\operatorname{length}_B\Delta_n\le r\,\mu_B(\Delta_n).
\]
Here \(\mu_B\) is the minimal number of module generators.

The exponent bound is sharp. For every field \(k\) and integer \(r\ge1\), put
\(B=k[[t]]\), \(R=B[[x,y]]\), \(I=(x,y)\), \(a=t^r\), and
\(J=(ax+y^2,ay+x^2)\). The two polynomials define distinct integral
carriers in \(\mathbf A^3_k\) and form a regular sequence in \(R\). With
\(X,Y\) the symbols of \(x,y\) in this example, the full initial ideals are
\[
H=(aX,aY,X^3-Y^3),\qquad
T=(aX^2,aXY,aY^2,X^3-Y^3).
\]
Here \(\Delta_3\simeq B/(t^r)\), all other degrees vanish, and
\(\operatorname{Ann}_B\Delta=\mathfrak a=(t^r)\). For \(r>1\),
\(t\) acts nontrivially on \(\Delta_3\).

[Complete proof in A-0010](../arguments/punctual_comparison_defects.md#the-conormal-entry-ideal-annihilates-the-defect)

## Limitations

In general \(\mathfrak a\subseteq\operatorname{Ann}_B\Delta\), and the
support of \(\Delta\) may be a proper subset of the zero set of
\(\mathfrak a\). The [supported quadruple](supported_quadruple_equation_filtration.md)
has simultaneous carrier singularity at the closed point and zero defect.
The DVR estimate controls the exponent of coefficient torsion in each
degree. It is vacuous for \(\mathfrak a=0\) and leaves the normal degrees
and the number of generators to be determined. The sharpness example
concerns a marked curve and gives no sole-support realization. The proof
uses normal codimension two.

## Review

The Phase 5 nonauthor reviewer `review_proofs` accepted the entrywise identities and cubic
sharpness route with R-0030. The reviewer reconstructed the actual Koszul
boundary and both identities modulo \(S_+H\) for arbitrary coherent \(J\)
in regular codimension two. They checked the rank-two Fitting presentation
without assuming a free source, the labelled carrier maps, and the DVR
bound. For the cubic family they checked irreducibility, all symbol
syzygies and their lifts over the possibly nonreduced coefficient quotient,
both full initial ideals, and the exact annihilator in every characteristic.
The review used symbolic arguments. The finite cubic replay is recorded
separately in A-0010.

punctual family without replaying it. The separate cubic family has its own
syzygy proof and three finite Rees-saturation samples in the source.
