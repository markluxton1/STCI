# Actual quartic normal symbols

## Scope

Over any field \(k\), fix \(C=C_0=[s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4]\subset\mathbf P^3_k\)
with ideal sheaf \(I\) and conormal \(E=I/I^2\). Put
\(Q=V(q)\) for \(q=x_0x_3-x_1x_2\), and choose its Segre factors so \(C\)
has class \((3,1)\). Write \(\mathcal O(n)=\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^1}(n)\).
Ambient twist \(4\) on \(C\) has parameter degree sixteen. Use the sheaf
powers \(I^i\) and the symbol maps
\[
\sigma_i:H^0(\mathbf P^3,I^i(4))
    \longrightarrow H^0(C,(\operatorname{Sym}^i E)(4)),\qquad i=1,2.
\]

## Statement

There are eighteen independent quartics through \(C\). Under the quotient
\(E(4)\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal O(10)\) from the quadric conormal
sequence, the first-symbol image is exactly the preimage of
\[
V_{10}=\operatorname{im}\bigl[H^0(Q,\mathcal O_Q(1,3))
    \longrightarrow H^0(C,\mathcal O(10))\bigr]
 =\langle1,u,u^3,u^4,u^6,u^7,u^9,u^{10}\rangle_k,\quad u=t/s.
\]
It has dimension seventeen inside the twenty-dimensional first-symbol
target. The kernel is \(H^0(\mathbf P^3,I^2(4))=kq^2\). The
second-symbol image is the line generated by the nonzero symbol of \(q^2\),
and \(H^0(\mathbf P^3,I^3(4))=0\). The second-symbol target has dimension
nine, with underlying bundle \(\mathcal O(2)^{\oplus3}\) if two is invertible
and \(\mathcal O(0)\oplus\mathcal O(2)\oplus\mathcal O(4)\) in characteristic
two.

On \(x_0=1\) put \(a=x_2-u^3,\ b=x_3-u^4\), using their classes in \(E\).
In characteristic two, in the split frame \(e=b-ua,\ f=a\), a section
\(A(u)e+B(u)f\) with coefficient degrees at most \(8,10\) respectively
is realized precisely when \(B_2=B_5=B_8=0\). If two is invertible,
in the balanced frame \(U=b-\tfrac32ua,\ V=a\), a section
\(P(u)U+R(u)V\) with coefficients of degrees at most nine is realized
precisely when \(P_1=2R_2,\ P_4=2R_5,\ P_7=2R_8\). Subscripts denote
coefficients of powers of \(u\).

## Limitations

The image conditions concern actual equations of ambient degree four and
their first two normal orders. They determine individual source symbols.
A pair realizing specified symbols still has to pass the higher-order
local intersection and global properness tests.

## Review

The Phase 5 independent reviewer accepted the quadric restriction route
using R-0020. The reviewer reconstructed the ambient lifts, the eight
restricted monomials, the coefficient tests in the balanced and split
frames, and the second-symbol line and target dimensions. Independent
enumeration of the 35 quartic monomials confirmed the first-symbol rank and
coefficient conditions over \(\mathbf Q,\mathbf F_2,\mathbf F_3,\mathbf F_5\)
and \(\mathbf F_7\). The geometric restriction and transition arguments
give the assertion over any field.
