# Normal Koszul differentials and the filtered equation defect

## Scope

Let \(R\) be a Noetherian local ring and \(J\subset I\) ideals. The
identification of minimal full initial equations with Tor holds with these
hypotheses. For the spectral sequence assume \(I=(x,y)\) is generated by a
regular sequence. Use either the finite filtration when \(I/J\) is
nilpotent, or the complete Noetherian \(I\)-adic setting with the shifted
adic filtration below. The latter setting allows a marked component of a
larger intersection. The construction works in arbitrary characteristic.
The transverse field assertion uses an \(I\)-primary two-equation complete
intersection in \(L[[x,y]]\), for a field \(L\).

## Statement

Write \(B=R/I\), \(M=R/J\), \(K=I/J\), and \(S=\operatorname{gr}_I R\).
Put \(H=\operatorname{in}_I(J)\), \(T=\operatorname{in}_I(IJ)\), and
\(A=\operatorname{gr}_K M=S/H\). Both initial ideals are full. Write
\(S_+=\bigoplus_{n>0}S_n\) and \(Q=H/S_+H\). There is a canonical graded
identification
\[
Q\simeq\operatorname{Tor}_1^S(A,B).
\]
It requires neither \(I\) nor \(J\) to be generated by a regular sequence.

For regular \(I=(x,y)\), let \(\mathscr K_\bullet=K_M(x,y)\), the
homological Koszul complex on the normal parameters. Give each normal
Koszul generator weight one, so that
\[
F^n\mathscr K_i=(K^{n-i})^{\binom{2}{i}},\qquad
K^a=M\quad(a\le0).
\]
For the stated filtration hypotheses this gives a convergent spectral
sequence, with \(i\) homological degree and \(n\) normal degree,
\[
E_1^{i,n}=\operatorname{Tor}_i^S(A,B)_n
\ \Longrightarrow\
\operatorname{gr}_F^n\operatorname{Tor}_i^R(M,B),
\qquad
d_r:E_r^{i,n}\longrightarrow E_r^{i-1,n+r}.
\]
If \(J=(f,g)\) is generated by a regular sequence, the underlying Tor
modules in degrees zero, one and two are \(B\), \(D=J/IJ\), and
\(\bigwedge_B^2D\), respectively.

In homological degree one the induced filtration under
\(\operatorname{Tor}_1^R(M,B)=D\) is
\[
F^nD=((J\cap I^n)+IJ)/IJ,\qquad n\ge1.
\]
Writing \(G_n=\operatorname{gr}_F^nD\), the quotient
\(Q_n=E_1^{1,n}\twoheadrightarrow E_\infty^{1,n}=G_n\) is the
comparison of [R-0030](local_filtration_comparison_defect.md).
In degree \(n\), take a graded cycle \((\bar a,\bar b)\) in the normal
Koszul complex and choose lifts \(a,b\in I^{n-1}\). The map to \(Q_n\)
sends the cycle to the class of \(xa+yb\bmod I^{n+1}\) in \(H_n/(S_+H)_n\).
One may change \(a,b\) by elements of \(I^n\) to arrange \(xa+yb\in J\);
then the map to \(G_n=H_n/T_n\) sends the cycle to the class of that
equation in \(F^nD/F^{n+1}D\).

For \(n\ge1\) set \(C_{0,n}=0\) and
\(C_{r,n}=\ker(Q_n\to E_{r+1}^{1,n})\) for \(r\ge1\). These modules
filter the defect, with
\[
C_{\infty,n}=\Delta_n=T_n/(S_+H)_n,\qquad
C_{r,n}/C_{r-1,n}
 \simeq\operatorname{im}\bigl(d_r:E_r^{2,n-r}\longrightarrow E_r^{1,n}\bigr).
\]
Only \(1\le r\le n-2\) can contribute. In particular
\(\Delta_1=\Delta_2=0\). If \(J\subset I^e\) for \(e\ge1\), then
\(\Delta_n=0\) through degree \(e+1\).

For \(R=L[[x,y]]\), \(I=(x,y)\), and \(J\) an \(I\)-primary ideal
generated by a regular sequence of length two, write \(h=\mu(H)\).
The incoming differentials remove exactly \(h-2\) pairs of dimensions
from homological degrees two and one, with a source in normal degree \(a\)
and a target in normal degree \(a+r\) on page \(r\). In this transverse
field case \(\Delta_n=0\) for \(n<4\).

## Limitations

The incoming images describe successive subquotients of the defect.
Over a curve these are modules over the actual curve ring \(B\).
A splitting of the filtration would require separate data. The first page uses
\(K_A(X,Y)\), where \(X,Y\) are the normal variables in \(S\). A filtered
carrier Koszul complex on \(f,g\) instead starts from the leading
carrier equations. The two constructions have different first pages.

The [degree-four field example](initial_equation_cancellation_counterexample.md)
attains the transverse lower bound. The
[punctual family with residual support](punctual_comparison_defect_family.md)
has degree-three defect over a curve. The comparison concerns local
equations. A supported, generically defect-free complete intersection
with a nonzero punctual defect remains an open case here.

## Review

The Phase 5 nonauthor reviewer accepted this route using R-0030. The reviewer
reconstructed the graded Tor identification, normal generator weights and
differential indexing, and convergence in the nilpotent and complete
Noetherian adic cases. For the adic case the review checked both the
Artin–Rees correction of approximate cycles and stabilization of boundary
symbols. With matching connecting signs, it checked on representatives
that the induced filtration is the equation filtration and the edge map is
the R-0030 quotient. The decision also covers the successive incoming
images, degree bounds and transverse-field Betti count. It is a mathematical
review of the local route.

The October 24 review independently reconstructed the Tor identification,
the distinction between the normal and carrier complexes, the induced
filtration on \(J/IJ\), the incoming subquotients and degree bounds. The
primary author supplied the explicit Artin–Rees convergence estimate in that
source. The October 26 audit addressed the local quotient comparison,
generic count, torsion criterion and smooth-carrier support. It left the
spectral-sequence details to the earlier source and recorded no fresh
executable replay or global sheafification check.
