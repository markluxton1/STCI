# The section defect of a sign-symmetrized actual source

## Scope

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from two,
let \(S=k[z,w,x,y]\), \(B=k[z,w]\), all variables of degree one, and
\(\tau(z,w,x,y)=(z,w,-x,-y)\). Let \(A=S/J\) be an actual
homogeneous ACM curve algebra whose reduced projective support is a union
of the translated quartics \(C_{u,v}\). A CI presentation is unnecessary.

## Statement

Put \(A'=S/\tau J\), \(K=J+\tau J\), \(Q=S/K\), and
\(T=K^{\rm sat}/K=H^0_{(z,w)}(Q)\). The invariant pure union and its
finite free section hull have canonical algebra descriptions
\[
 E=S/(J\cap\tau J)=A\times_Q A',\qquad
 N=E^{**}_B=A\times_{Q/T}A'.
\]
Their section/Rao defect is \(N/E\simeq T\), with a sign twist in the
equivariant comparison:
\[
                      N/E\simeq T\otimes\operatorname{sgn}.
\]
If the union cycle has nonzero first moment, some degree-\(d\) class in
the \(B\)-head \(T/(z,w)T\) has natural sign \((-1)^d\); the section
hull has opposite-sign generators in some equal degree.

In characteristic zero a source with sole support \(C_{u,v}\) always
has such a union moment, so \(J+\tau J\) must be nonsaturated. For a
CI this is the ideal generated by the parity parts of its two equations.
This identifies a defect from the actual source rather than an arbitrary
free replacement.

[Proof, controls and interpretation](../arguments/torus/sign_symmetry_and_actual_sources.md).

## Limitations

Nonsaturation can already follow from low-degree equations in the saturated
intersection; its existence alone is not an additional obstruction.
In the sector \(K^{\rm sat}=I_C\), even the signed hull profile is forced
by the CI degrees. Along punctured parity homotopy the whole parity-part quotient
and torsion can stay fixed while the source differential varies. Retain
the marked factors and maps and the section-hull multiplication; a torsion
or head-only calculation is not a sufficient discriminator in these controls. For several
components the symmetrized union's moment need not equal the original
moment. No unrestricted CI or ACM-source exclusion follows.

## Review

A distinct reviewer independently reconstructed the union and fiber-product
identities, depth and saturation arguments, extended algebra structure and
equivariant sign twist in the current contribution. The parity consequence
uses the inherited sign-invariant matrix theorem and section-head theorem.
This review does not assert a general bound on the resulting torsion or
its compatibility with arbitrary CI degeneration.
