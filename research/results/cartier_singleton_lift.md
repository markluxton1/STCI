# Cartier singleton-lift classification

## Statement

Let \(k\) be algebraically closed of characteristic zero, let \(C\subset\mathbf P^3\) be a smooth rational curve of degree \(d\), and let \(X\) be an integral degree-\(a\) hypersurface containing \(C\) with an **actual set-theoretic mate**. Let \(\nu:S\to X\) be its finite normalization and \(H=\nu^*\mathcal O_X(1)\). Suppose the **full reduced inverse image** is a single curve \(c\), \(\nu|_c:c\to C\) is an isomorphism, and \(c\) is Cartier on \(S\).

Then \(|c|\) identifies \(S\) with a **normal surface of minimal degree**, and exactly the following models occur as necessary possibilities:

| \((S,c)\) | \(H\) | \(a\) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \(\mathbf P^2\), \(c\) a line | \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2}(d)\) | \(d^2\) |
| \(\mathbf P^2\), \(c\) a smooth conic; \(d\) even | \(\mathcal O_{\mathbf P^2}(d/2)\) | \(d^2/4\) |
| Smooth rational normal scroll of degree \(n\ge2\), \(c\) a hyperplane section, \(n\mid d\) | \((d/n)c\) | \(d^2/n\) |
| Rational-normal cone of degree \(n\ge2\), \(c\) a smooth Cartier hyperplane section, \(n\mid d\) | \((d/n)c\) | \(d^2/n\) |

Here \(n=c^2=d^2/a\). The table lists **necessary normalization/polarization models**, not constructed STCI examples.

## Hypotheses and quantifiers

The Cartier condition is **upstairs on S**, not on the possibly nonnormal carrier \(X\). Generic regularity of \(X\) along \(C\) is not an additional hypothesis once the full singleton degree-one lift is assumed. No claim is made for non-Cartier lifts, several inverse branches, or inverse curves mapping to \(C\) with degree greater than one.

## Proof outline

Because \(c\) is Cartier and smooth, the normal surface \(S\) is regular along \(c\). The mate gives \(m c\sim bH\), making \(c\) ample, with \(c^2=d^2/a>0\). Resolving singularities away from \(c\) and using adjunction and surface classification yields rationality and \(H^1(S,\mathcal O_S)=0\). The exact Cartier restriction sequence gives \(h^0(S,\mathcal O_S(c))=n+2\) and basepoint-freeness. The resulting finite birational morphism realizes \(S\) as a normal surface of minimal degree. The singular extension is the rational-normal cone, whose Cartier polarization is generated by \(c\). See the original theorem and independent audit for the detailed argument.

## Consequences for the fixed C0

For \(C_0=[s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4]\), the accepted all-integral-quartic exclusion [STCI-R-0001](quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) removes the degree-four entries. Exactly three **higher-degree necessary models within these hypotheses** remain:

| Carrier degree | Normalization \(S\) | Lift and polarization |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | \(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) | \(c=(1,1),\ H=(2,2)\) |
| 8 | Quadric cone | \(c\) a smooth Cartier conic, \(H=2c\) |
| 16 | \(\mathbf P^2\) | \(c\) a line, \(H=\mathcal O(4)\) |

## Limitations

The actual projection parameters, complete inverse support, and full conductor descent for the three fixed-\(C_0\) models **remain unresolved**. The classification does not establish existence of a mate on any listed carrier. The unrestricted STCI problem for \(C_0\) remains open.
