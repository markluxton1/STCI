# Finite determinacy, uniqueness, and the distinct root-tower positivity regimes

Two different positivity regimes occur in the root literature. For a moving pure power m^r with direction line M of degree -e, the first-relation correction bundle is Q_k=H^(-k)⊗M^(-(r+k+1))⊗Sym^(r-1)V. On P1 it is O((r+k+1)e−lambda*k)^r. Vanishing of H1 constructs compatible **first-principal-relation** jets, but the final truncation ideal has extra generators and need not be Gorenstein or two-generated. The later coprime relation has a separate degeneration cost. This is a constructive formal mechanism, not evidence that all first relations are obstructed.

For a fixed finite-type family of full roots of class(epsilon,r), the [uniform tower proof](../../arguments/spectral/uniform_root_towers_and_inseparable_bridges.md#2026-10-15-generalization-uniform-root-tower-finite-determinacy) instead uses lambda>3epsilon to make every H0 correction space zero, even on singular/nonreduced roots. Here epsilon is the coefficient-map degree of the whole first relation; for a pure power it equals r*e. Uniqueness lets every obstruction stage be a closed subscheme of **one** finite-type coefficient space. Noetherian stabilization then gives an existential finite stage for that fixed family. It does not produce its value, and one equality of adjacent stages does not prove there are no later equations. For C0 and epsilon=r=2 the obstruction ranks are12m−8, so ranks4 and16 are the first two stages of this single tower.

The [intrinsic finite-family proof](../../arguments/spectral/finite_root_families_and_smooth_obstructions.md#2026-10-15-generalization-intrinsic-finite-root-family) removes a split-normal presentation by using a smooth proper family Z/S of connected Cartier root curves and the actual line N_(Z/E)⊗O_E(-mE)|Z. If nu=deg O_E(E)|Z exceeds max(Z²_E,0), its degree Z²_E−m*nu is negative for allm≥1. Relative perfect cohomology then retains uniqueness over nonreduced parameter bases. Smoothness is essential to that degree argument; it is a different sufficient hypothesis from the balanced rational theorem that permits nonreduced roots.

The common mechanism is uniqueness of **embedded ideals**, with equation units treated as presentation changes. A formal stable locus can be nonempty; to exclude it one needs a further argument such as smooth-root algebraization and incompatible degree bounds. To construct an STCI one still needs actual global two-equation realization and sole support. These are separate uses of the finite-tower result, so both constructive and obstructive directions remain visible.

The prime-to-p root restriction is proved directly in [the full spectral owner](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_torsion_and_syzygy_splitting.md#prime-to-characteristic-spectral-descent-excludes-repeated-moving-components). It implies that an actual saturated moving-pure-power generic-CI presentation on the rational base, if it exists in characteristic \(p\), must have its later initial-generator degree s divisible by \(p\). The [distinct apolar shortcut](../../arguments/spectral/moving_power_second_relation.md#positive-characteristic-reach-of-the-pure-power-mechanism) applies to arbitrary smooth projective base curves when \(p>s\). These are reviewed source-level extensions, not changes to R-0009/R-0011 scope.

The [intrinsic arithmetic owner](../../arguments/arithmetic/arithmetic_root_collision.md#embedded-primitive-towers-gluing-arithmetic-variation-and-actual-pairs) connects this discussion to three broader embedded mechanisms: the primitive-stage obstruction hierarchy with its fixed lower ideal, comparison of two marked quadratic neighborhoods by their actual derivations/transition difference, and defect-twisted vanishing forced by an actual equation pair. Its counterexamples show that generic primitivity need not globalize and that the same conormal bundle can support different embedded obstruction classes. These are comparisons of marked neighborhoods and actual source maps; the conormal splitting alone is not their entire input. The arithmetic divided-power interpretation retains its stated comparison hypotheses.
