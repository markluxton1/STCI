## Conditions before a transformation

For **each** proposed move, first record the exact Result statement and hypotheses, complete source section range, proof method and whether it is independent or merely a lemma/audit, all incoming links and Result dependencies, proposed target path, and retrieval advantage. If those cannot be established, the action remains **retain and link**, not split. No changes to mathematical source files are authorized by this document.
