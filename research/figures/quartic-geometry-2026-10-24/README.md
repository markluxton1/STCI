# Rational quartic geometry figures

Date: 2026-10-24. These are illustrations accompanying the provisional
research investigation, not independent mathematical evidence or canonical
results.

- [`projection-center.svg`](projection-center.svg) and
  [`projection-center.png`](projection-center.png): a structural schematic
  of the complete quartic embedding, its projection center, and the smooth
  projected curve on its unique quadric. The drawn spatial positions are
  schematic; no metric or affine model of projective four-space is asserted.
  The source and target are respectively `P(W^vee)` and `P(V^vee)`.
- [`symmetric-square-conics.svg`](symmetric-square-conics.svg) and
  [`symmetric-square-conics.png`](symmetric-square-conics.png): actual sampled
  real equations in the affine chart `alpha+gamma=1`. With
  `x=alpha-gamma` and `y=beta`, the diagonal is the circle
  `x^2+y^2=1`, and the bitangent conic is the hyperbola
  `x^2-2y^2=1`. Both are tangent to `x=-1` and `x=1` at their
  intersections. The picture illustrates algebraic results over an
  algebraically closed field of characteristic zero; it does not restrict
  those results to the real field.

The quadratic `h=alpha*s^2+beta*s*t+gamma*t^2` represents its zero divisor.
Thus `x=-1` represents `h=t^2` and the divisor `2p_0`, while `x=1`
represents `h=s^2` and `2p_infinity`, with
`p_0=[1:0]` and `p_infinity=[0:1]`.

Both figures are generated by
[`draw_quartic_geometry_2026_10_24.py`](../../computations/draw_quartic_geometry_2026_10_24.py).
Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 research/computations/draw_quartic_geometry_2026_10_24.py
```

SVG generation uses only the Python standard library. PNG generation uses
Pillow when available; otherwise the script writes SVGs and explicitly
reports that PNGs were skipped. The checked desktop runtime has Pillow
12.3.0:

```sh
/Users/markluxton/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/bin/python3 \
  research/computations/draw_quartic_geometry_2026_10_24.py
```

The PNG renderer uses Arial Unicode/Arial Bold on macOS, DejaVu Sans on
Linux when available, and a Pillow default fallback otherwise. The SVGs
use ordinary text and vector paths, so they remain editable and scalable.
The generated PNGs were visually inspected at 1500 by 900 pixels; the
initial overlapping source label and clipped intersection label were
corrected in the retained version.
