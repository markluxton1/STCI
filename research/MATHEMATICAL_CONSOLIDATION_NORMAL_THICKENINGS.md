# Mathematical consolidation: normal thickenings and characteristic jump

**Working thematic assessment; not theorem promotion.** The organizing question is: when can a local transverse thickening of the fixed rational quartic be realized by two global homogeneous surfaces with no residual components?

## Mathematical spine

1. **Embedded geometry.** R-0020 establishes the conormal splitting: O(-7)^2 in characteristic zero, O(-8) plus O(-6) in characteristic two. This affects global first-symbol realization; splitting alone does not produce a complete intersection.
2. **Actual equations versus first symbols.** The characteristic-dependent integral pair in the quartic-characteristic-conormal note gives a sharp test: over characteristic zero it has four distinct quartic components; in characteristic two they coalesce to a single quadruple structure. In characteristic two the first symbols alone generate (X), while cancellation of actual equations produces the fourth-order generator Y^4. Thus an argument using only first normal symbols can miss the defining thickness.
3. **Intrinsic local rank-four algebra.** The independently reviewed local-algebra note classifies relative Gorenstein rank-four algebras with generic K[z]/z^4 under its hypotheses: R[A,B]/(A^2-t^d B,B^2). For d>0 the embedded ideal becomes (x^2-t^d y,y^2); for d=0 it becomes (v,u^4). This uses the saturated Frobenius pairing and characteristic-zero division by 2; it is not a characteristic-free classification.
4. **Defect divisor and ambient contact.** In that rank-four setting, the first-normal fixed divisor is exactly D2=D3. The defect d measures a change from primitive to embedding-dimension-two special fibre. The local classification connects filtered algebra to singularities of a general surface in a pencil, but the resulting K3 Picard-rank proof is a separate global argument, not the preferred organizing principle for new research.
5. **Global realization and limits.** R-0010 normal flatness requires saturated actual first relation and full ordinary generic CI hypotheses. R-0030 comparison defect is a local diagnostic, not a global obstruction. R-0032 supplies a numerical socle budget, not an exclusion. R-0022 proves the characteristic-zero (4,4) exclusion with inherited inputs; R-0001 already excludes every quartic carrier with arbitrary mate degree.

## What deserves an authoritative home

- A filtered-algebra chapter with the Frobenius pairing, saturated versus ordinary filtration, local normal forms and defect divisors.

## Important failed shortcuts to retain

- Treating dependent first symbols as the entire initial ideal: false in the characteristic-two pair; actual cancellation yields Y^4.
- Treating local normal flatness or a numerical socle identity as global nonexistence: missing realization and/or positivity bridges.
- Treating the char-two model as an unramified lift to char zero: R-0012 gives a prescribed arithmetic nonlifting obstruction; the flat integral pair lifts as a scheme but splits into residual components.
- Treating every exceptional vertical curve as a residual component downstairs: invalid without an actual residual-cycle argument.
- Treating a (4,4) obstruction as a proof for higher degrees: not justified.
