# Mathematical statements, evidence, and proof review

A claim is assessed through its exact statement, hypotheses, proof, and the
scope of its review. There is no separate class of authoritative Results and
no additional acceptance step after a proof review. File location, identifier,
branch, recency, catalog inclusion, and merge approval supply no mathematical
justification.

## Statements and proofs

A Result contains its exact mathematics, hypotheses, quantifiers, and limitations.
Its complete proof is in that document or a precisely identified Argument
section containing the actual reasoning. Other exposition may explain an
application, but cannot broaden the statement through paraphrase.

Separate alternative proofs, each with its jointly required premises. A
conditional statement retains its antecedents. A necessary condition, a finite
reduction, and a construction are different mathematical assertions; none
supplies an unfinished computation or an unproved realization step.

## Scoped reviews

Each proof route records an accepted, pending, or disputed review and a precise
passage explaining what was checked, which assumptions it used, what was read
or executed, and any remaining qualification. Acceptance applies to that scope;
it does not accept every statement in the surrounding file.

An independent reviewer reconstructs consequential arguments, checks hidden
assumptions and counterexamples, and compares proposed removals with the
replacement mathematics. Source review and independent reconstruction can be
described factually, without assigning either a separate Result classification.
Do not infer a proof review from a discarded administrative label.

The structural checker follows accepted routes and their accepted prerequisites,
allowing separate alternatives and rejecting circular justification. It checks
declared structure, not mathematical truth, completeness of premises, or the
sufficiency and independence of a review. An unreviewed or disputed route cannot
justify a reviewed conclusion. The same rules apply to every record format.

## Corrections and open questions

When statements conflict, inspect both scopes and proofs; a newer timestamp or
more prominent page does not settle the conflict. Record the exact disputed
step and retain a useful corrected statement or counterexample. Explain a scope
bridge before replacing one statement by another.

Keep actual missing implications, unperformed computations, and unresolved
questions explicit. In particular, the unrestricted characteristic-zero STCI
question remains open unless an independently reviewed proof resolves it.
Navigation pages, graph relationships, and successful scripts cannot supply
that proof.

## Retention and integration

Retain useful intermediate lemmas, complete arguments, constructions,
counterexamples, precise open questions, and the smallest necessary exact
support. Review replacements and removals together. Git records earlier
versions; administrative queues, repeated certificates, and historical wrappers
have no independent retention requirement.

The [model](MODEL.md) specifies objects and their proof routes. The
[catalog](CATALOG.md), [reading routes](INDEX.md), and [mechanism map](MAP.md)
help find the mathematics. PR approval governs repository integration and is
separate from the mathematical scope of a proof review.
