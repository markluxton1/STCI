# A complete five-parameter order-two projection slice is inadmissible

October 29, 2026. Investigator: projection_incidence/five_param_slice.
Status: **reviewed provisional proof**, with the
[independent audit](2026-10-29-projection-five-parameter-audit.md).
This excludes the literal family below, not the full sixteen-parameter
admissible projection locus.

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Write the
source smooth quadric in symmetric coordinates as
\[
S=V(ad-y^2+z^2/4)\subset\mathbf P^3,\qquad c=V(z).
\]
Interchanging the two factors of \(S=\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\)
fixes \(a,y,d\) and sends \(z\) to \(-z\). Retain all five scalar
parameters in
\[
\begin{aligned}
U_0&=a^2+h_0z^2,&U_1&=a(y+\gamma z)+h_1z^2,\\
U_2&=d(y-\gamma z)+h_2z^2,&U_3&=d^2+h_3z^2.
\end{aligned}\tag{1}
\]
On \(c\), set \((a,y,d)=(s^2,st,t^2)\), obtaining the literal
curve \(C_0=[s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4]\). This is the complete slice
\(A=B=D=E=0\) of the nine-parameter normal form in
[the residual geometry note](2026-10-29-projection-residual-geometry.md).
Both middle coordinates and every \(h_i\) vary.

**Claim.** No member of (1) is basepoint free, birational onto its image,
and has full reduced inverse support of \(C_0\) equal to \(c\).
For \(\gamma\ne0\), even basepoint freedom and full inverse support alone
are impossible. All boundaries, including \(\gamma=\pm1/2\), are retained.

## Determinant and the residual contact degree

For \(q=x_0x_3-x_1x_2\), direct calculation on \(S\) gives
\[
q(U)=z^2R,
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
R={}&(\gamma^2-1/4)ad+h_3a^2+h_0d^2
-h_2a(y+\gamma z)-h_1d(y-\gamma z)\\
&+(h_0h_3-h_1h_2)z^2.
\end{aligned}\tag{2}
\]
Its restriction is
\[
R|_c=h_3s^4-h_2s^3t+(\gamma^2-1/4)s^2t^2-h_1st^3+h_0t^4.
\tag{3}
\]
If this is zero, all \(h_i=0\) and \(\gamma^2=1/4\); then (1) has
basepoints at \(a=d=0,\ y=\pm z/2\). Thus a basepoint-free member has
residual divisor \(D=V(R)\) of type \((2,2)\), with \(c\not\subset D\).

On the target quadric \(Q=V(q)\), use ruling coordinates
\[
x_0=A_0B_0,\quad x_1=A_0B_1,\quad
x_2=A_1B_0,\quad x_3=A_1B_1.
\]
The curve is the Cartier divisor
\(A_1B_0^3-A_0B_1^3=0\) of class \((1,3)\).
For a basepoint-free tuple with determinant divisor \(2c+D\),
the two ruling pullbacks on the full residual \(D\) have degrees two
and six. Hence the section cutting out \(C_0\) on \(D\) has degree
twenty, whereas \(D\cdot c=4\).
This is the reviewed calculation in
[the projection audit](2026-10-29-projection-locus-audit.md),
including reducible and nonreduced \(D\).

Assume full inverse support equal to \(c\). No component of
\(D_{\mathrm{red}}\) maps into \(C_0\). A Cartier divisor on the smooth
surface \(S\) is Cohen--Macaulay with no embedded associated points.
The target-\(C_0\) section is therefore a nonzerodivisor on \(D\), and
its zero scheme has length twenty, supported on \(D\cap c\).
We will bound that length by six.

## Ordinary diagonal points contribute their intersection length

On \(a=1\), use regular source coordinates \(y,z\), with
\(d=y^2-z^2/4\). On the target chart \(x_0\ne0\), the curve ideal is
generated by \(q\) and \(H_0=x_0^2x_2-x_1^3\). Along the diagonal,
\[
[z]H_0(U)=-4\gamma y^2.\tag{4}
\]
For \(\gamma\ne0\) this is nonzero at every finite diagonal point
except \(p_0=(1,0,0,0)\). The pulled-back ideal consequently equals
\((z)\) at each such point. The only other possible exceptional point
is \(p_\infty=(0,0,1,0)\), outside this chart.

At every point of \(D\cap c\) other than these endpoints, the zero
scheme of the target-\(C_0\) section on \(D\) is thus exactly
\(D\cap c\), including arbitrary intersection multiplicities and
nonreduced residual structures.

## Endpoint support forces transverse residuals and contact two

By (3), \(p_0\in D\) exactly when \(h_3=0\). Then full inverse support
and basepoint freedom force \(h_2\ne0\). If \(h_2=h_3=0\), work off
the diagonal in \(z=1\). On the two ruling fibers
\[
d=0,\qquad y=\varepsilon/2,\qquad\varepsilon\in\{1,-1\},
\]
one has \(U_2=U_3=0\) and
\(U_1=a(\varepsilon/2+\gamma)+h_1\).
The two coefficients of \(a\) differ by one, so at least one is
nonzero. Choosing \(a\) to make \(U_1=0\) produces a basepoint if
\(U_0=0\), or an extra inverse point of \([1:0:0:0]\) otherwise.

For \(h_3=0,\ h_2\ne0\), expansion at \(p_0\) gives
\[
R=-h_2(y+\gamma z)+O((y,z)^2),\qquad
H_0(U)=h_2z^2+O((y,z)^3).
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(D\) is smooth and transverse to \(c\), with \(z\) a
uniformizer. Its target-\(C_0\) contact is exactly two, while
\(D\cap c\) has length one there.

Directly at \(p_\infty\), membership in \(D\) is equivalent to
\(h_0=0\). Full inverse support forces \(h_1\ne0\): otherwise the
off-diagonal fibers \(a=0,\ y=\varepsilon/2,\ z=1\) have
\(U_0=U_1=0\) and \(U_2=d(\varepsilon/2-\gamma)+h_2\). One can
make \(U_2=0\), producing a basepoint or an extra inverse point of
\([0:0:0:1]\). For \(h_0=0,\ h_1\ne0\), in the chart \(d=1\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
R&=-h_1(y-\gamma z)+O((y,z)^2),\\
H_\infty(U)&=-h_1z^2+O((y,z)^3),\qquad
H_\infty=x_2^3-x_1x_3^2.
\end{aligned}\tag{6}
\]
Again \(D\) is smooth and transverse to \(c\), with contact exactly two.

Every ordinary point contributes its diagonal intersection length.
Each endpoint, if present, replaces length one by length two. The total
target-\(C_0\) contact on the full residual scheme would therefore be
\[
4+\#\{p_0,p_\infty\text{ that belong to }D\}\le6,
\]
contradicting twenty. This excludes every \(\gamma\ne0\) member
without assuming birationality.

If \(\gamma=0\), all four coordinates are invariant under the
nontrivial factor-interchange involution of \(S\). Any basepoint-free
map then has generic degree at least two and cannot be birational.
This completes the five-parameter slice.

## Evidence and scope

The [exact verifier](../computations/stci_projection_incidence_five_2026_10_29.m2)
retains all five parameters and checks (2), (4), and both endpoint
congruences. Its
[saved output](../computations/stci_projection_incidence_five_2026_10_29.txt)
contains no parameter samples. The contradiction is the uniform
Cartier-contact proof. The
[independent audit](2026-10-29-projection-five-parameter-audit.md)
reconstructs the scheme-length and endpoint arguments with separate
exact controls.

The four further antisymmetric parameters of the full order-two source
move its ramification quartic away from the two fixed endpoints used
here. They are not classified by this proof. The order-one source and
the surviving descent problems remain open. No canonical record is
changed and no unrestricted STCI conclusion is asserted.
