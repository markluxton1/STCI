## 1. Authority and the inherited conductor mechanism

The characteristic-zero nilpotent logarithm criterion and characteristic-\(p\) annihilation are already in the source of R-0003, [conductor theorem](2026-10-13-generalization-conductor-theorem.md), §§1,8. The new interface correctly identifies them as inherited.

For a finite injective map of Artinian conductor algebras containing \(\mathbb Q\), the nilpotent-unit kernel is identified by finite logarithm with a rational vector group. Multiplication by a nonzero integer is injective there. A unit whose reduced phase is torsion therefore descends after some power exactly when its actual logarithmic defect vanishes; torsion of its reduced phase alone is insufficient. In characteristic \(p\), a sufficiently large \(p\)-power kills the nilpotent kernel of a fixed finite algebra. Neither statement supplies the required global divisor or carrier.

R-0003 concerns a **fixed** integral CM projective carrier with finite normalization and the full inverse support, including its isolated-point condition. R-0004 has additional actual finite birational projection-family and Cartier-singleton hypotheses. Neither reduction computes the admissible phase images for \(C_0\). The proposed note retains these boundaries.

## 2. Reconstruction of the all-\(N\) local proposition

Let \(B=K[[t]]\), \(N\ge2\), and \(A_N=K+t^NB\).

The conductor is \(t^NB\): that ideal is contained in \(A_N\) and remains so after multiplication by every element of \(B\); an element of \(A_N\) with nonzero constant term has a product with \(t\) outside \(A_N\). These exhaust the possibilities in \(A_N\). The displayed complete semigroup ring description is correct, since every exponent at least \(N\) is generated by \(N,\ldots,2N-1\). Thus this is a genuine finite-normalization one-branch complete local domain, not only an abstract quotient of unit groups.

For \(m=p^eM\), \(p\nmid M\), the first nonconstant term of
\[
(1+t)^m=(1+t^{p^e})^M
\]
is \(Mt^{p^e}\), with nonzero coefficient in \(K\). Membership in \(A_N\) is consequently equivalent to \(p^e\ge N\). The least positive integer with this property is the least power of \(p\) at least \(N\). In characteristic zero, the coefficient \(m\) of \(t\) is nonzero, so no positive power descends.

The quantifier distinction is also exact. In characteristic \(p\), each truncation \(B/(t^N)\) makes \(1+t\) torsion of a sufficiently large \(p\)-power order, whereas no nonzero power equals one in \(B\). The exponents cannot be chosen uniformly as \(N\) grows. This does not contradict R-0003, which uses the finite generic conductor algebra of one finite-type carrier, rather than the entire tower with growing length.

No sampled calculation is needed for this all-\(N,m,p\) argument. It is not promoted into a projective construction.

## 3. Semi-invariant powers: valid lemma and one missing finiteness hypothesis

For a finite group acting on a domain \(B\), if \(g(f)=\chi(g)f\) and the character has order \(r\), then \(f^r\in B^G\). For the invariant ring \(A=B^G\), the inclusion \(A\subset B\) is integral: every element is a root of its orbit polynomial. Lying over then identifies the image of \(V_B(f)\) with \(V_A(f^r)\). Thus the zero-support claim is correct.

The source note calls this a **finite** quotient while assuming only that \(B\) is a normal domain. To assert finiteness literally, add that \(B\) is finite over \(A\), for example by taking \(B\) to be a finitely generated \(k\)-algebra with finite-group action. Without that hypothesis the proof still gives an integral quotient and the support assertion; it should not silently assert module finiteness. This is a minor statement-level correction, not a failure of the proposed projective mechanism.

In a projective application, normality, the appropriate line-bundle linearization, invariant section descent, and identification with an ambient twist must be supplied separately. An arbitrary affine semi-invariant or quotient has not constructed a hypersurface in \(\mathbf P^3\).

## 4. Classical twisted-cubic control

The [benchmark audit](2026-10-27-char0-torsion-benchmark-audit.md) supplies independent primary-source provenance for the classical pair. I independently reconstructed its polynomial identities:
\[
Q=x_0x_2-x_1^2,\quad
G=x_2^3-2x_1x_2x_3+x_0x_3^2,
\]
\[
x_0G=(x_0x_3-x_1x_2)^2+x_2^2Q,\qquad
x_2G=(x_1x_3-x_2^2)^2+x_3^2Q.
\]
Both identities hold with the mixed coefficient \(2\); they are not Frobenius identities. They show reduced support equal to the twisted cubic, and the proper degree-six intersection has generic multiplicity two on that degree-three curve.

On \(x_3=1\), the quadric cone's affine coordinate ring is \(k[u^2,uv,v^2]\). In characteristic zero its finite sign quotient has the anti-invariant \(u-v^3\), whose square is exactly \(G\). This verifies the stated mechanism. It is a different curve from \(C_0\), and its index-two geometry cannot bypass R-0035's bounded-index boundary for \(C_0\).

## 5. Cancellation in the relation cone

For two actual relation vectors as specified in the source, the corresponding regular normalization sections multiply, their divisors add, and the full off-\(C\) conductor classes multiply. On the preimage of torsion reduced phases the characteristic-zero logarithmic defect is a homomorphism. Opposite defects therefore cancel, while the residual phases remain torsion. Full positivity of the combined divisor allows the R-0003 descent criterion to apply.

The local control \(u=1+\epsilon\), \(v=1-\epsilon\), \(\epsilon^2=0\), is exact: neither nonzero logarithmic defect is killed by an integer power in characteristic zero, but \(uv=1\). Its role is a conditional mechanism. It supplies no two global sections on an admissible carrier.

The note correctly identifies the global limitation: on a normal integral projective variety over an algebraically closed field, two sections of the same line bundle with the same divisor differ by a scalar. Scalar replacement cannot adjust the conductor unit class. Repeated powers along a single divisor ray multiply a nonzero logarithmic defect; cancellation requires genuinely compatible global divisor directions, not freely prescribed local units.

## 6. Primary literature scope refreshed

I freshly opened [Hartshorne--Polini, §7](https://arxiv.org/html/1301.3222). Proposition 7.1 requires finite intersection with the singular locus for APic proportionality. Proposition 7.3 treats a smooth curve with nonzero local APic torsion and gives a singleton normalization fiber; Corollary 7.4 adds ordinary singularities and restricts intersection to pinch points. Lemma 7.6 is the characteristic-zero torsion-freeness of \(\operatorname{Pic}(X)/\mathbb Z[H]\). These are precisely the restrictions used in the note; none gives a universal exclusion of non-Cartier high-index or nonnormal carrier constructions.

The nodal-product pinching control also reconstructs: an ample curve in the normalization meets one conductor fiber, and full inverse support forces the paired distinct point into that curve. Its map to a smooth marked image cannot then be an isomorphism. This is an abstract projective control, not a hypersurface construction.

The separate [discrepancy-literature assessment](2026-10-27-absorption-discrepancy-literature.md) records a related distinction between reduced support and the full possibly nonreduced primary scheme; it does not modify the conductor arguments above.

## 7. Outcome and retained missing bridge

The local exponent proposition, finite/formal warning, semi-invariant support lemma, corrected-square control and conditional defect cancellation pass independent reconstruction. The finite-quotient phrase needs the explicit hypothesis in §3. All main construction implications remain conditional on actual compatible global sections, the full marked inverse support and an admissible carrier.

For \(C_0\), no new carrier or supported pair has been produced. The useful general research interfaces are a corrected semi-invariant power in the proper ambient twist, or a fully positive conductor relation with cancelling actual logarithmic defects. These are mechanisms to seek, rather than a list of surface/mate-degree cases.
