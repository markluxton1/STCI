The October 2026 theorem assumes a smooth complex projective threefold
\(X\) with \(H^1(O_X)=0\), smooth connected \(C\), ordinary thickening
\(I_Z=I_C^q\), \(q\ge2\), and a very general sufficiently high-degree
surface \(Y\supset Z\). It computes \(\operatorname{Pic}(Y)\) from
\(\operatorname{Pic}(X)\). Such carriers are nonnormal along \(C\).
This line-bundle statement does not compute every almost-Cartier divisor,
the full inverse support, or a sole-support defining pair. It is neither
an unrestricted STCI exclusion nor a construction for this fixed quartic.

The linked-resolution application uses Kleppe's theorem to separate
ordinary residual Hilbert deformations from the stratum preserving the
low-degree equations. All new formulas and their independent reconstruction
are in the [liaison source](2026-10-28-residual-liaison-construction.md) and
[audit](2026-10-28-residual-liaison-audit.md), not inferred from a literature
abstract. No claim about every residual curve with matching invariants is
made.

## Repository duplication checks and authority

Each lane followed INDEX → MAP → smallest relevant source/result. The
all-degree general residual and Rao calculation, cubic image and graph
proof, large-twist partial transfer, full-primary attachment formula,
formal lifting and twisted-cubic torsion benchmark were found before new
work and retained as inherited inputs. In particular the October 21
cubic-image source already identified \(V_5\); it was not rediscovered.

The present contributions are exact applications and refinements: every
attachment divisor in a sufficient range; sharp ideal-sheaf-power
cohomology; the linked residual's equation recovery and deformation
interpretation; the two-projection residual dictionary and fixed
anticanonical conductor; and actual prescribed-index carriers with explicit
local residual cancellation. Searches did not establish literature
originality of these exact formulations. They remain reviewed provisional
repository proposals.
