# The missing linear coordinate is already obstructed at first order

Date: 2026-10-30. Status: **independently reviewed provisional**, with the
[independent proof audit](2026-10-30-four-streams-linear-series-audit.md).
No canonical promotion and no STCI exclusion.

Work over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero. Put

\[
C=[s^4:s^3t:st^3:t^4]\subset P=\mathbf P^3,\qquad
L=\mathcal O_P(1),\qquad e=s^2t^2\in H^0(C,L|_C).
\]

Write \(I=\mathcal I_C\), \(E=I/I^2\), and
\(X=C^{(1)}=V(I^2)\), the **first infinitesimal neighborhood**, of
generic multiplicity three. Degrees inside \(\mathcal O(d)\) below are
degrees on \(C\simeq\mathbf P^1\), so \(L|_C=\mathcal O(4)\).

The exact conclusion is that the missing-section condition cannot give
the proposed contradiction on the branch where both equations vanish
to order at least two along \(C\). Its nonzero class is already forced
on \(X\), and survives in both full torus limits and their pure hulls.
There is also an arbitrary-multiplicity family of torus-invariant,
locally complete-intersection multiple curves on \(C\) with exactly four
linear sections. Thus even adding local Gorensteinness does not force the
missing coordinate to lift.

## 1. Sources, inherited inputs, and what is new here

The promoted input is
[R-0020](../results/quartic_conormal_characteristic_jump.md):
\(E=\mathcal O(-7)^2\) in characteristic zero. The projection geometry
and the graph of \(e\) in \(\operatorname{Tot}(L)\) were already developed
in [the resolved-projection note](2026-10-24-p4-resolved-projection.md),
Sections 1, 3–4. Neither is claimed as new here.

The primary literature was checked before choosing this route:
[Bayer–Eisenbud, *Ribbons and their canonical embeddings*](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~bayer/papers/Ribbons_BE95.pdf),
Sections 1 and 4, treats embedded ribbons and section lifting; its
appendix proves the rational-normal-curve normal-bundle formula used
below. [Gallego–González–Purnaprajna, *Smoothable locally Cohen–Macaulay
and non Cohen–Macaulay multiple structures on curves*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5458)
explains why first-order deformation data do not generally determine the
whole multiple structure. No theorem about smoothing or closedness of
the Cohen–Macaulay/Gorenstein locus is used here.

The contribution is the exact identification of the **particular**
missing-coordinate class with the graph-normal extension, its consequence
for full limits and pure hulls, and the all-multiplicity control in
Section 4. These are applications of established geometry rather than
a claimed new general theory. They resolve a suggested bridge in
[the degeneration note](2026-10-30-four-streams-degeneration.md),
Section 5, in the direction that prevents that bridge from being an
obstruction to the entire-thick branch.

## 2. The graph-normal extension is the missing-section obstruction

Let

\[
D=[s^4:s^3t:s^2t^2:st^3:t^4]\subset\mathbf P^4,
\qquad z=[0:0:1:0:0].
\]

Projection from \(z\) identifies \(D\) with \(C\). The open space
\(\mathbf P^4\setminus\{z\}\) is \(\operatorname{Tot}_P(L)\), and
\(D\) is the graph of \(e\) over \(C\). The relative tangent line
of this bundle gives the normal sequence

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathcal O(4)
\longrightarrow N_{D/\mathbf P^4}
\longrightarrow N_{C/P}\longrightarrow0.                 \tag{1}
\]

The rational normal quartic has normal bundle \(\mathcal O(6)^3\).
Dualize (1) and tensor by \(L|_C\) to obtain

\[
0\longrightarrow E\otimes L|_C=\mathcal O(-3)^2
\longrightarrow\mathcal O(-2)^3
\longrightarrow\mathcal O_C\longrightarrow0.              \tag{2}
\]

**Proposition 2.1.** Up to the harmless sign convention for Čech
coboundaries, the extension class of (2) is

\[
\delta_X(e)\in H^1(C,E\otimes L|_C),
\]

where \(\delta_X\) is the connecting map of

\[
0\longrightarrow E\otimes L|_C
\longrightarrow L|_X\longrightarrow L|_C\longrightarrow0.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]

Consequently \(\delta_X(e)\ne0\) and
\(h^0(X,L|_X)=4\). Under any splitting \(E=\mathcal O(-7)^2\),
the tensor \(\delta_X(e)\in k^2\otimes H^1(\mathcal O(-3))\)
has rank two.

**Proof.** On open sets of \(P\) trivializing \(L\), choose local
extensions \(e_i\) of \(e\). The differences \(e_i-e_j\) modulo
\(I^2\), with the transitions of \(L\) understood, are the Čech
cocycle defining \(\delta_X(e)\). In the total space of \(L\), let
\(w\) denote the fiber coordinate in the corresponding trivialization.
The graph ideal is locally generated by \(I\) and \(w-e_i\).
The classes of \(w-e_i\) lift the generator of the relative conormal
line. On overlaps, their differences are \(-(e_i-e_j)\). These are
exactly the off-diagonal transition terms of the graph conormal sequence.
After the indicated twist this is (2), proving the comparison.

This argument does **not** view \(\mathcal O_X\) as an
\(\mathcal O_C\)-algebra or assume a retraction \(X\to C\).
Sequence (3) is an exact sequence of sheaves on \(P\); its left
term is supported on \(C\). The graph conormal sequence is a sequence
of vector bundles on \(C\), and the explicit cocycle compares their
cohomology classes.

For a coordinate check, on \(x_0=1\) put

\[
u=x_1,\qquad a=x_2-u^3,\qquad b=x_3-u^4.
\]

Local lifts of \(e\) on the two endpoint charts are
\(x_1^2/x_0\) and \(x_2^2/x_3\). Their difference, in the frame
\(x_0\) and modulo \((a,b)^2\), is

\[
u^2-\frac{(u^3+a)^2}{u^4+b}
 =-2u^{-1}a+u^{-2}b.                                      \tag{4}
\]

Thus (2) measures the actual omitted coordinate, not an unidentified
nontrivial extension with the same bundle ranks and degrees.

Since \(H^0(\mathcal O(-2)^3)=0\), the connecting map applied to
\(1\in H^0(\mathcal O_C)\) in (2) is nonzero. This proves the
nonvanishing. The four ambient linear forms lift their restrictions to
\(X\), span a codimension-one subspace of \(H^0(L|_C)\), and
\(H^0(E\otimes L|_C)=0\). Sequence (3) therefore gives
\(h^0(L|_X)=4\).

Finally, if the tensor class had rank at most one, a constant change
of the two summands of \(\mathcal O(-3)^2\) would make one extension
component zero. Its middle bundle would then have an
\(\mathcal O(-3)\) direct summand. This contradicts the middle
bundle \(\mathcal O(-2)^3\): every morphism
\(\mathcal O(-2)^3\to\mathcal O(-3)\) is zero. \(\square\)

Geometrically, restoring the fifth coordinate would lift the projection
graph to this infinitesimal neighborhood. The obstruction is already
first order, and occupies both balanced normal directions. There is no
need to invoke extension through every formal neighborhood to prove it.

The same four-section statement holds on every ordinary infinitesimal
neighborhood \(V(I^r)\), \(r\ge2\). Indeed the successive kernels
after twisting by \(L\) are
\(\operatorname{Sym}^jE\otimes\mathcal O(4)
=\mathcal O(4-7j)^{j+1}\), with no global sections for \(j\ge1\).
Thus the section spaces inject backwards into \(H^0(X,L)\), while
the four ambient sections extend to every order. This says nothing
about the normal layers of an arbitrary multiple curve.

## 3. The class necessarily survives both limits on the entire-thick branch

For any \(C\subset W\subset P\), write
\(\mathcal N_W=\ker(\mathcal O_W\to\mathcal O_C)\) and let
\(\delta_W(e)\in H^1(\mathcal N_W\otimes L)\) be its section-lifting
class.

**Theorem 3.1.** Let \(Y\subset P\) have reduced support \(C\) and
\(\mathcal I_Y\subset I^2\). Let a one-parameter subgroup of
\(\operatorname{Aut}(P)\) preserve \(C\), and let \(Z\) be either
full projective Hilbert limit of its translates of \(Y\). If
\(T\subset\mathcal O_Z\) is the maximal zero-dimensional subsheaf
and \(\mathcal O_{\overline Z}=\mathcal O_Z/T\) is the pure hull,
then

\[
X\subset\overline Z\subset Z,\qquad
\delta_Y(e)\ne0,\quad\delta_Z(e)\ne0,\quad
\delta_{\overline Z}(e)\ne0.                              \tag{5}
\]

In particular this holds for both directions of the torus with ambient
weights \((0,1,3,4)\). It holds for every hypothetical supported
complete intersection whose two equations vanish to order at least two
along \(C\), with no degree or multiplicity bound.

**Proof.** The invariance of \(C\) implies the invariance of its
first infinitesimal neighborhood \(X\). Every translate of \(Y\)
therefore contains the same fixed \(X\). The scheme-theoretic Hilbert
limit also contains \(X\). For an ideal-level verification, use the
**saturated** homogeneous ideal \(K=H^0_*(I^2)\) of \(X\).
It is homogeneous for the one-parameter subgroup. If the saturated
homogeneous ideal of \(Y\) is \(J\), then \(J\subset K\) implies
\(\operatorname{in}_{\pm w}(J)\subset K\). Saturating the full
initial ideal preserves this inclusion because \(K\) is saturated.
This uses the full initial ideal, not just two initial equations.

The sheaf \(\mathcal O_X\) has no zero-dimensional subsheaf. Indeed,
locally \(C\) is a regular codimension-two embedding, and
\(\mathcal O_X\) is locally the quotient by \((x,y)^2\). A
parameter along the smooth curve is a nonzerodivisor on this quotient;
equivalently, the layers \(\mathcal O_C\) and \(I/I^2\) are
locally free over the curve. Thus \(X\) is Cohen–Macaulay and pure.
Under the surjection \(\mathcal O_Z\to\mathcal O_X\), the image
of \(T\) would be a zero-dimensional subsheaf of \(\mathcal O_X\),
so is zero. The surjection factors through
\(\mathcal O_{\overline Z}\), proving \(X\subset\overline Z\).

Whenever \(X\subset W\), the restriction map
\(\mathcal N_W\otimes L\to E\otimes L|_C\) carries
\(\delta_W(e)\) to \(\delta_X(e)\) by naturality of connecting
maps. Proposition 2.1 now proves all nonvanishings in (5).
\(\square\)

The condition \(\mathcal I_Y\subset I^2\) is exactly that its
first normal image vanish. If the two global equations have normal
order at least two at the generic point of \(C\), their first
symbols, which are sections of vector bundles on the integral curve,
vanish identically; hence the required sheaf inclusion holds everywhere.

This theorem closes a specific uncertainty about specialization. On this
entire-thick branch, the missing class survives **because the fixed
first neighborhood survives**, independently of the endpoint torsion.
It supplies no contradiction to a supported complete intersection.
Any argument claiming that an invariant pure hull must lift \(e\)
would already be contradicted by the invariant pure curve \(X\).
For a pair with a nonzero first normal image, the theorem makes no such
claim; its full higher extension data remain relevant.

## 4. A locally complete-intersection control in every multiplicity

Let \(Q=V(x_0x_3-x_1x_2)\simeq\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\).
Choose the ruling labels so that \(C\) has class \((1,3)\) and
\(L|_Q=\mathcal O_Q(1,1)\). For every integer \(n\ge2\), let
\(W_n=nC\) as a Cartier divisor on \(Q\), embedded in \(P\).

**Proposition 4.1.** Each \(W_n\) is torus invariant, has reduced
support exactly \(C\), and is locally a complete intersection, hence
Cohen–Macaulay and Gorenstein. Nevertheless

\[
h^0(W_n,L|_{W_n})=4,\qquad \delta_{W_n}(e)\ne0.             \tag{6}
\]

None of these curves is arithmetically Cohen–Macaulay: for example,

\[
h^1(P,\mathcal I_{W_n}(n))=2n-1.                           \tag{7}
\]

**Proof.** The Cartier divisor \(nC\) on the smooth hypersurface
\(Q\) is locally given in \(P\) by a regular sequence
\((q,f^n)\). Both \(Q\) and \(C\), hence the divisor subscheme
\(nC\), are invariant under the indicated torus. Its reduced support
and multiplicity are immediate from this local equation.

The divisor restriction sequence at twist one is

\[
0\to\mathcal O_Q(1-n,1-3n)\to\mathcal O_Q(1,1)
\to L|_{W_n}\to0.
\]

For \(n\ge2\), both entries of \((1-n,1-3n)\) are negative,
so its \(H^0\) and \(H^1\) vanish by the cohomology of
\(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\). Therefore restriction induces
an isomorphism \(H^0(Q,\mathcal O_Q(1,1))\simeq H^0(W_n,L)\).
These are the four ambient sections, whose restrictions to \(C\)
omit \(e\), proving (6).

For completeness, the ideal sequence at any ambient degree \(d\) is

\[
0\to\mathcal O_P(d-2)\to\mathcal I_{W_n}(d)
\to\mathcal O_Q(d-n,d-3n)\to0.
\]

The intermediate cohomology of line bundles on \(\mathbf P^3\)
vanishes, so at \(d=n\) its first cohomology is
\(H^1(Q,\mathcal O_Q(0,-2n))\), of dimension \(2n-1\).
This proves (7). \(\square\)

This family is a control, not a proposed family of STCI equations.
It shows geometrically that a complete linear series on the support
can fail to extend to a pure Gorenstein thickening of any multiplicity.
Local Gorensteinness, torus invariance, and the correct degree-one
section count do not enforce ACM. The missing ACM information in these
examples appears at higher twists, exactly as (7) exhibits.

## 5. Scope, failed implications, and review boundary

The two controls address different potential hidden assumptions.
The first neighborhood \(X\) is pure and invariant but is not locally
Gorenstein; the curves \(W_n\) are invariant and locally Gorenstein,
but are not ACM. Neither is a counterexample to the unrestricted STCI
question. No retraction from an arbitrary multiple curve to \(C\),
closedness of a Gorenstein locus, preservation of normal Hilbert
functions, or equality between homogeneous degree pieces and all
sheaf sections has been assumed.

For an actual complete intersection with both equation degrees at least
two, ACM already gives \(h^0(\mathcal O_Y(1))=4\), hence nonvanishing
of \(\delta_Y(e)\). Theorem 3.1 shows that this particular requirement
also holds automatically in both full limits and pure hulls whenever
the first normal image vanishes. Therefore forcing it to vanish cannot
be the desired universal bridge on that branch. A successful global
argument must use additional information, such as the interacting
cohomology in higher degrees or the actual two global generators;
this note proves no implication from those data to nonexistence.

The unrestricted characteristic-zero STCI status is unchanged. The
result is a decisive barrier to a proposed geometric implication and
an exact persistence theorem, not another carrier or degree exclusion.

Independent review must reconstruct: the graph-cocycle comparison;
the saturated ideal containment through both limits; the pure-hull
factorization; and the distinction between (6) and full ACM in (7).
The proofs use no numerical computation or inherited proof rerun.
