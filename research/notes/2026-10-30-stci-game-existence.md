## 1. Response to the common-cubic failure

The [reviewed quadric-boundary theorem](2026-10-30-stci-game-quadric-boundary.md)
does more than reject one coefficient choice. In its fixed-cubic source
all higher-normal-order corrections vanish on the unique quadric
\(Q\) containing \(C_0\). They cannot change the residual ruling divisor
on \(Q\). Increasing their dimension or the mate degree does not solve
that geometric problem.

The source proposed as E2 in the ledger therefore needs genuinely
moving normal data without a common global cubic. Individual symbol
surjectivity supplies such lifts, but does not supply a supported pair.
There is also a reason not to start with a generic nonsplit ordinary node
and equal degrees \((8,8)\): if its quadratic normal symbol is saturated,
contact on both conjugate branches makes the two quadratic symbols
scalar multiples. After an actual scalar subtraction, a degree-eight
mate has normal order at least three. The reviewed row filter then
forces a quadric factor and retains residual support. This observation
has the stated saturated/nonsplit hypotheses; it does not exclude the
whole shared-moving-factor source.

My stronger independent move is E3 below. It changes the geometry of
the inverse curve, rather than only the coefficients of a rational
diagonal lift.

## 2. E3: an elliptic inverse curve of degree two over C

Work over \(\mathbf Q(i)\subset k\), where \(i^2=-1\) and \(k\) is
algebraically closed of characteristic zero. Let
\[
E:\ y^2=x^3-x,\qquad O=\text{the point at infinity},\qquad
T=(i,1-i).
\]
The tangent at \(T\) shows that \(2T=(0,0)\), so \(T\) has order four.
Put
\[
M=\mathcal O_E(4O),\qquad L=\mathcal O_E(O+T).
\]
Then \(\deg M=4,\deg L=2\), and
\[
L^4=M^2,\qquad L^2\ne M.
\]
The difference \(L^2M^{-1}\) is a nonzero two-torsion line bundle.
This is intrinsic data, not a coefficient count: the degree-two pencil
\(L\) and the degree-four embedding bundle \(M\) fit together only after
squaring.

Embed \(E\) by \(|M|\), using coordinates
\[
(Z_0:Z_1:Z_2:Z_3)=(1:x:y:x^2).
\]
Its actual homogeneous ideal is generated by
\[
Q_0=Z_0Z_3-Z_1^2,\qquad
Q_1=Z_2^2-Z_1Z_3+Z_0Z_1.
\]
Choose a smooth member
\[
S_\mu=V(Q_1+\mu Q_0)
\]
of this quadric pencil. It is a smooth quadric surface, hence a form of
\(\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1\) over the algebraic closure. On it the
marked curve
\[
\Gamma=V(Q_0|_{S_\mu})
\]
has class \((2,2)\), is elliptic, and is linearly equivalent to the
polarization \(H=\mathcal O_{S_\mu}(2,2)\).

The degree-two function
\[
A=\frac{y+1-i}{x-i}
\]
has pole divisor \(O+T\), so it is the pencil \(L\).
Let
\[
\phi=\frac{(y+(1+i)x)^2}{x}
=x^2+2ix-1+2(1+i)y.
\]
The tangent-line divisor gives
\(\operatorname{div}(\phi)=4T-4O\). Consequently
\[
\phi,\quad \phi A,\quad \phi A^3,\quad \phi A^4
\]
are actual sections of \(M^2=\mathcal O_E(8O)\).
Their map on \(E\) is precisely
\([1:A:A^3:A^4]\), including the pole points after changing frame.
Thus \(\Gamma\to C_0\) has degree two.

This is outside [R-0002](../results/cartier_singleton_lift.md):
that classification assumes an inverse curve mapping **isomorphically**
to \(C_0\). It is also outside the previous marked diagonal source,
where the inverse curve was rational of class \((1,1)\).
The degree-two inverse is deliberate; neither classification may be
silently extended to it.

## 3. A literal five-parameter global family

The coefficient rows are

These are exact global homogeneous equations. The

For each smooth \(S_\mu\), take the full four-parameter lift
\[
U_j=V_j+\rho_j Q_0,\qquad j=0,1,3,4.
\tag{1}
\]
There are five parameters \(\mu,\rho_0,\rho_1,\rho_3,\rho_4\).

It does not exhaust all elliptic curves, degree-two pencils, or surface
normalizations.

A basepoint-free member gives a finite morphism because its pullback

it covers the degree-four target curve twice.

The nontrivial two-torsion above prevents an automatic use of the former

\]
Only the two members \(\mu=\pm i\) are invariant; \(S_2\) is not.
Birationality must be tested on the actual surface map; it cannot be

## 4. The first actual member: geometrically viable, then rejected

The exact computation takes
\[
\mu=2,\qquad \rho_0=\rho_1=\rho_3=\rho_4=0.
\]
It establishes:

1. \(S_2\) is smooth.
2. The four quadratic sections have no projective basepoint.
3. The kernel of their ring map has one homogeneous generator of
   degree eight. The image therefore has degree eight, and the finite
   map has generic degree \(8/8=1\). It is an actual normalization of an
   integral degree-eight surface.
4. Full inverse support fails. After adding \(Q_0-1\), the pullback
   ideal of all four standard generators of \(I_{C_0}\) is
   zero-dimensional of degree twenty-four.

The last number has a geometric normalization. Since \(Q_0\) has degree
two, \(Q_0=1\) is a free \(\mu_2\) cover of the projective complement

therefore has length twelve. Distinctness of twelve geometric points is
not asserted. It is an isolated extra inverse scheme, not a residual
curve on the source.

This single image has no mate in any degree. A mate would pull back to
a nonzero section whose zero support included \(\Gamma\) and these
isolated points, while a Cartier zero divisor on the smooth surface
cannot have isolated zero points outside its curve support.
This rejection does not require the birationality calculation, but the
latter confirms that the new higher-degree inverse model itself occurs

## 5. The bounded construction test and the existence response

e_0+e_1+e_3+e_4=b\}
\tag{2}
\]
in \(H^0(S_\mu,bH)\).
Indeed \(\Gamma\sim H\), so a section with sole zero divisor \(b\Gamma\)
is a scalar multiple of \(Q_0^b\).
Conversely a positive identity (2) constructs the actual ambient equation
and itself excludes extra inverse points over \(C_0\).

The first degree passing the numerical low-degree exclusions is
\(b=5\), corresponding to \((8,5)\) and target generic intersection

The degenerate-leading locus is not discarded: two normalization
branches can have a common tangent with higher contact.

The existence strategy therefore should test \(b\ge6\), or first
identify a degenerate-leading parameter locus, rather than spend time

## 6. What this changes about the general STCI question

The positive information is a new actual normalization source outside
the currently classified degree-one inverse models. The restrictive
two-torsion geometry is explicit and the four target coordinates are
global quadrics. Thus the surviving higher-degree inverse possibility
is a concrete construction space, not merely a logical exception.

The negative information is equally concrete: basepoint freedom,
birationality, and the correct marked curve map still leave a length-twelve
isolated inverse defect. Removing this defect is a separate geometric
task. A full-family exclusion would close this specified construction,
not every elliptic inverse model or every presentation of \(C_0\).

The unrestricted question therefore remains open. The next useful
outcome is an actual parameter with no isolated inverse support and a
descending power, or an independently reviewed obstruction on this
whole five-parameter source.
