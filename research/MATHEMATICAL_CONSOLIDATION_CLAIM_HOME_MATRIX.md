### Editorial rule for overlapping chapters

Use a short self-contained explanation of the result's *relevant aspect* in each subject, then link to the canonical statement. Example: R-0020 belongs to G for the embedded conormal geometry, A for the characteristic jump, and F for consequences for global thickening. Avoid repeating full proofs, and do not conflate the different hypotheses of nearby results.
