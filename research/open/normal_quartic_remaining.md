# Normal quartic carriers — historical partial frontier

Status: **CLOSED for integral quartic STCI mates of fixed C0 in characteristic zero** by [STCI-R-0001](../results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md). The material below describes a historically incomplete classification, not an active mate frontier.

The surviving normal quartic frontier consists of rational-resolution Ishii–Nakayama B1/B2/B3/D cases with reducible, singular, or nonreduced exceptional anticanonical divisor.

Already excluded are rational singularities, positive-genus ruled irrational cases, the case where \(C\) avoids the nonrational exceptional anticanonical locus, and the simple-elliptic case.

## Next target

Treat the exceptional anticanonical divisor as a finite lattice/divisor problem. Combine
\[
c+Z\equiv H,\qquad K_M=-E,
\]
with \(c\cdot E\), the first-normal singularity budget, and the Cartier-index/denominator bounds.

A useful intermediate result would reduce survivors to explicit combinatorial types for \(E\) together with prescribed intersections with the strict transform of \(C\).
