# Jet-condition / discrepancy framework

Status: **STRUCTURAL RESEARCH QUESTION — quartic-carrier subcases superseded**

## Problem

For primitive or quasiprimitive filtrations of C, study the successive ambient-section restriction maps, their maximal-rank defects, and the special-point divisors modifying their targets. The numerical condition budget alone does not control dependence among jet conditions.

## Established framework and location

The definitions of target shortfall and maximal-rank defect, the conormal comparison divisor R, the dimensions of the first quartic source, and the finite second-jet evaluation on 2R are now summarized in [order-one structure](../results/order_one.md). The full original calculations remain in the [October 6 jet-discrepancy framework](../notes/2026-10-06-order-one-jet-discrepancy-framework.md) and [e=1 rebuild](../notes/2026-10-07-e1-jet-rebuild.md). The separate [quartic jet geometry](../results/jet_geometry.md) records the e=2 geometric interpretation.

The accepted [quartic-carrier exclusion](../results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) supersedes every historical question asking whether a quartic carrier can have an STCI mate, including the e=0 and e=1 order-one cases. Those old cases are not the current existence frontier. Their intermediate rank calculations and exceptional strata remain useful as models, not as open quartic-mate obligations.

## Surviving research question

For actual carrier degrees at least five, and separately for residual intersections where a quartic may still occur, determine when the successive normal-jet conditions become dependent after accounting for saturated layer divisors and actual global section sources. A useful result would describe rank-defect loci intrinsically and compare them with punctual defects of the actual equation module, with hypotheses explicit.

The broader interfaces are in [normal filtrations and boundary obstructions](../synthesis/normal_filtration_boundary_obstructions.md) and [formal normal intersections](../synthesis/formal-normal-intersection.md). Neither a generic rank calculation nor a first-normal-factor calculation proves an unrestricted STCI obstruction. In particular, the e=1 comparison-zero direction requires separate treatment; the naive fixed-line argument in the historical rebuild was corrected by a Cartier intersection argument.

## Guardrails

Do not infer nonexistence from a negative expected condition budget. Do not transfer the e=2 rank-collapse mechanism to e=0 or e=1, or treat a historical quartic-mate survivor as a present open case. Preserve original exact certificates and independent audits until claim-level coverage is verified.
