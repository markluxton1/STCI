# Entirely-thick branch

Status: **OPEN — priority 2**

Both defining equations have generic normal order at least two. This branch is not covered by the order-one Bănică–Forster bounds.

## Proved necessary conditions

For degrees \(a,b\) and exact generic normal orders \(p,q\ge2\),
\[
ab\ge4pq,\qquad 7ab+28pq\ge16(aq+bp),
\]
and
\[
a\ge3p,\qquad b\ge3q.
\]
The normalized \((3,3)\) boundary is excluded. The \((3,4)\) equality boundary forces repeated-root geometry.

## Main obstruction to the current method

First tangent forms do not determine transverse intersection length: higher contact can be arbitrarily large with fixed first-symbol data.

## Next target

Replace first-symbol data by successive-transform/Rees-valuation data. Start on equality and near-equality cases of the numerical inequality and seek a finite invariant forcing either an extra common curve or forbidden normalization/conductor support.
