# Mathematical status

For the fixed embedded rational quartic \(C_0\), unrestricted STCI existence
in characteristic zero remains **open**. The [result records](results/INDEX.md)
own promoted statements and exact hypotheses. The linked mathematical proof
homes also retain reviewed provisional results and conditional reductions;
consolidation alone does not promote them.

The maintained account also describes proper intersections containing C0,
their multiplicities and residual cycles, supported multiple structures, and
normalization, projection and deformation constructions. These are mathematical
outcomes in their own right; unrestricted non-STCI is not a presumed conclusion.

## Established boundaries

- [R-0001](results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) excludes every integral
  quartic carrier with any mate for this embedding in characteristic zero.
  Together with the lower-degree and factor arguments, both defining degrees
  of a hypothetical characteristic-zero STCI are at least five.
- [R-0022](results/quartic_44_filtered_exclusion.md) excludes a quartic pair
  independently. Its mechanisms are actual filtered contact, the local-singularity/global-surface contradiction, and the [intrinsic fourth-extension obstruction](synthesis/intrinsic_fourth_extension.md). Direct blowups remove local singularity classification from the surface presentation. The new embedded-neighborhood proof instead computes the contact-extension class and uses no global surface bound; its scope remains the fixed characteristic-zero quartic pair.
- In every characteristic \(p>0\), the same fixed curve has an STCI
  presentation with a quartic carrier. [Explicit constructions and fixed-carrier
  degree restrictions](supplements/POSITIVE_CHARACTERISTIC_CONSTRUCTIONS.md)
  explain why the characteristic-zero exclusion cannot be transferred wholesale.
- The [full-conductor criterion](results/fixed_carrier_conductor_criterion.md)
  is a fixed-carrier equivalence. Necessary normalization models and structured
  phase reductions do not constitute computed existence or exclusion for all
  higher-degree carriers.

## Reviewed contributions with bounded scope

The current proof homes make the scope explicit: saturation and actual-source
conditions for [filtrations](synthesis/03-infinitesimal-geometry.md);
actual examples and family-dependent [residual bounds](synthesis/residual_intersections_and_jets.md);
[elliptic polarization, actual sections and node gluing](synthesis/projection_and_elliptic_gluing.md);
[partial marked absorption](synthesis/marked_deformation_and_absorption.md);
and [full contact/endpoint models](synthesis/contact_duality_and_global_descent.md).
They support constructive and obstructive investigations without resolving the
unrestricted question.

The [quintic–octic theorem](synthesis/quintic_octic_surface_constraints.md)
excludes a proper sole-supported degree-(5,8) pair whose quintic has only
rational double points everywhere, including away from C0. Reducedness of
the first-symbol divisor is no longer assumed. The full cover and symbolic-triple
proof includes repeated symbols; the earlier reduced-symbol even-node proof
remains a separate useful argument. The full (5,8) problem remains open.

The [nonnormal quintic theorem](synthesis/quintic_gorenstein_normalizations.md)
excludes every mate degree for an integral nonnormal quintic with Gorenstein
normalization. It treats integral, reducible and nonreduced conductors. Normal
quintics and non-Gorenstein normalizations are outside it; the
[toric source calculation](synthesis/quintic_non_gorenstein_toric_sources.md)
retains actual models and projection constraints for the latter. Its
[complete center calculation](synthesis/quintic_toric_center_incidence.md)
excludes the one-infinity boundary and all but the specified M incidence
in the finite chart. Emptiness of that remaining locus is unproved, and a
survivor would still need actual power descent. The general
[conductor–differential theorem](synthesis/carrier_conductor_differential_comparison.md)
forces a normalization singularity on the inverse curve or a singular full
conductor contact for a generically regular integral carrier of degree at least
five admitting a sole-supported mate. Its local singular-gluing controls explain
the smoothness boundary.

The [ruled-surface criterion](synthesis/ruled_surface_degree_criterion.md) now includes a sharp characteristic-free genus inequality and an integral degree-gap refinement. It unifies the low-sectional-genus quartic and quintic rationality reductions and gives a conditional degree-ten rationality application; it is not a new STCI exclusion. The [sextic source-rank analysis](synthesis/rank_strata_and_sextic_lifts.md) determines the single rank-drop root and proves uniqueness of compatible low-order actual sextic lifts on the necessary root open, without deciding the remaining nonlinear incidence.

The [quadratic-cover account](synthesis/quadratic_covers_and_conductor_descent.md) retains arbitrary-pair boundary formulas separately from excluded projected families, and generalizes the graded conductor inequality to a homogeneous module generator of degree d over a two-dimensional Gorenstein ring. [Punctual extensions](synthesis/punctual_quadric_extensions.md) exist at every finite order but do not supply actual homogeneous columns. [Supported basic-link operations](synthesis/supported_linkage_operations.md) have a uniform generic type-two failure gate under their stated local hypotheses. The [complement section account](synthesis/complement_section_obstructions.md) proves that the checked continuous and primary characteristic-class conditions impose no further degree restriction beyond divisibility; actual section realization remains open. Its [embedded extremal-case argument](synthesis/quadratic_covers_and_conductor_descent.md#12-embedded-curve-exclusion-of-the-minimal-boundary) strengthens the necessary boundary length to \(\delta\ge a+b-2\) for a hypothetical characteristic-zero fixed-quartic pair. Equality in the previous bound would force a residual line; no incompatible upper bound is known.

The [projected-thickening theorem](synthesis/projected_thickening_section_defects.md) strengthens the explicit source failure from non-AG to non-ACM for independent powers and homogeneous binary mixtures with nonzero degree product in the field. Its actual missing section and Rao lower bounds do not persist automatically under reduction or transfer to arbitrary primary lifts. Known ACM schemes upstairs on the quadric threefold do not settle the weaker ACM-thickening problem downstairs in P3.

For a hypothetical characteristic-zero sole-support pair, let \(\delta\) be
the finite quadratic conductor-boundary length. The [ambient quadratic norm](synthesis/quadratic_covers_and_conductor_descent.md#81-ambient-quadratic-norms-force-conductor-postulation)
requires no conductor-boundary equation below \(q=\lceil a/2\rceil-1\)
and gives \(\delta\ge a+b-3+2[\binom{q+2}{3}-q]\) for
\(5\le a\le b\). This is stronger than the earlier scalar bound in this
application. It uses the same canonical inequality with additional ambient
information and supplies no upper capacity bound. The conductor's full section
ring, quadratic action and graph relations retain different data; completing
sections is not an embedded ACM repair.

The [elliptic double-inverse construction](synthesis/elliptic_double_inverse_construction.md)
provides actual global quadratic sections and a verified finite birational octic
member. That member has an extra projective inverse scheme of length twelve and
therefore no mate in any degree. The five-parameter family's full inverse-support
and descent loci remain open. The separate balanced degree-ten discriminant
chart now has independent computational review; the degree-twelve chart is
unfinished. Neither target classification nor one failed member decides the
whole elliptic source.

The [reduction for arbitrary sole-support sextic pairs](supplements/SEXTIC_PAIR_REDUCTION.md)
retains three necessary divisor profiles. In characteristic zero it gives normal
pencil carriers, an index-nine point, a nontrivial order-three Weil class, and
necessary invariants of its normal cyclic cover. It also excludes a reduced
common first-normal divisor while retaining local nonreduced controls. The
remaining global sextic realization problem is open; the cover's invariants are
not those of an assumed smooth resolution, and no contradictory upper irregularity
bound is known. The [ADE refinement](supplements/SEXTIC_ADE_REDUCTION.md)
leaves three primitive-triple orbits under its global ADE pencil hypothesis.
The actual 27-dimensional sextic source and its first contact equation are
computed; higher contact, properness and sole support still need proof.

The [polarized retraction obstruction](synthesis/contact_jacobians_and_hamiltonian_actions.md#a-pencil-compatible-polarized-retraction-cannot-extend-globally)
is a characteristic-free section-count result, with a separate punctured-cover
application when two is invertible. It describes a restriction on how the
multiple structure can sit over the reduced curve, not an STCI exclusion.

The [full torus-limit argument](synthesis/global_equations_and_constructions.md#the-supported-hilbert-locus-and-its-full-torus-boundary)
preserves reduced support C0 and Hilbert polynomial through the containments
\(I_{C_0}^N\subset I_Z\subset I_{C_0}\). In characteristic zero, an invariant
homogeneous CI ideal would admit weight-homogeneous generators and is excluded
by the [weight theorem](supplements/EQUIVARIANT_DEGREE_BOUNDS.md). These reviewed
provisional facts distinguish invariant reduced support, an invariant full
ideal, and the two separately limiting equations. They do not exclude a
supported CI whose full invariant limit loses its CI structure. Classification
of the relevant invariant limits and recovery of CI or ACM structure in
deformations preserving support remain open.

The provisional [constant-quotient descent criterion](synthesis/toric_quotients_and_endpoint_descent.md)
recovers the saturated orbit-family image from the embedded algebra and tests
prescribed CI degrees by actual homogeneous source spaces and properness.
The same abstract algebra need not have the same projective image. The
specified length-four nilpotent embedding has infinity indices 13, 15 and 18
in characteristic zero, three and two, respectively; only the characteristic-two
image is the prescribed quartic CI. Its flat characteristic-zero collision has
genus 28 and is not that genus-33 CI family.

The provisional [sextic configurations and full limits](synthesis/toric_sextic_residuals_and_limits.md)
give a sharp quotient-length bound of eight for every proper pair of eigenform
sextics, in every characteristic, hence boundary cycle degree at least four.
Explicit mixed-weight proper intersections exhibit residual splitting,
absorption into C0 and a saturated ACM torus limit with a necessary degree-ten
generator. These examples have residual support; they provide no sole-support
CI deformation or unrestricted sextic exclusion. Recovering the known
23-dimensional double source in quotient coordinates is a reformulation, while
the length-nine conormal and divisor restrictions still need full embedded
multiplication and moving-family data. No canonical result is promoted here.

The [spectral power theorem](synthesis/spectral_pushforward_powers.md) now
contains the general genus-one pushforward lemma and all-power smooth (2,2)
coefficient maps. Its [determinant scheme](synthesis/spectral_determinant_schemes.md)
is the relevant relative Picard torsion scheme and supports universal graded
complete-intersection families, including over nonreduced bases. The
[fourth-power application](synthesis/spectral_torsion_and_syzygy_splitting.md)
retains its sixteen-dimensional obstruction target and actual fixed-coordinate
operator. Graded existence does not imply actual equations: an explicit
order-three example fails the first ambient operator. The
[torsion-two calculations](synthesis/quadratic_root_torsion_two_lifting.md)
exclude the covered pure-direction and linear-rank-drop boundaries; the
remaining rank-two charts and global realization are unresolved.

The [order-three parameterization](synthesis/order_three_roots_and_sextic_lifts.md)
covers the full smooth torsion locus in fixed ruling frames. Its completed
affine four-parameter slice has 24 smooth first-compatible geometric points:
22 fail the actual sextic-symbol test, and two fail the complete cubic lift.
This excludes that slice, not the full seven-dimensional family. The
[actual source comparison](synthesis/actual_octic_root_lift_comparison.md)
gives a sextic map with source dimension at most three and target dimension
eleven. On exact-order-three roots, later sextic membership is automatic
if compatible primitive root lifts exist; those lifts are still an obligation.
The same account constructs a proper (8,8) pair with generic length eight
along C0 and residual curve-cycle degree 32, an actual containing intersection
which does not have sole support.

The [global jet account](synthesis/global_equation_jets.md) gives an intrinsic
source-deficit filtration, a general finite homogeneous realization theorem,
an explicit quadric-flag cutoff, and the exact sextic minimum cutoff of order
five. Its octic correction recursion retains the actual allowed source images.
[Bounded certificates](synthesis/bounded_equation_certificates.md) distinguish
verification of an actual fixed-degree pair from finding a pair or proving an
effective all-degree witness bound. The
[pencil Milnor bound](synthesis/pencil_milnor_budget.md) remains a useful general
identity, but for the fixed quartic it follows from stronger local geometry.

[Effective characteristic transfer](supplements/EFFECTIVE_CHARACTERISTIC_TRANSFER.md)
gives an unconditional growing lower bound for quartic-carrier mate degrees;
growth for all defining pairs is conditional on characteristic-zero nonexistence.
[Trace](supplements/MIXED_CHARACTERISTIC_TRACE_OBSTRUCTION.md),
[prime-to-p extraction](supplements/PRIME_TO_P_POWER_EXTRACTION.md) and
[Frobenius precision](supplements/FROBENIUS_POWER_LIFTING_PRECISION.md) constrain
prescribed supported lifts and actual traits with their stated precision and
ramification hypotheses. They do not obstruct unrelated characteristic-zero
presentations. The prescribed powered-quartic specialization additionally
forces positive lower-root defect at both endpoints in any hypothetical
supported trait. Compatibility of that defect with the actual
complete-intersection algebra is unresolved.

The [Grassmannian Hom identity](synthesis/chow_bundle_maps.md) shows in every
characteristic that a map of the prescribed Sylvester bundle types already
is a pair of ambient equations. The missing datum is such a map with the
required determinant. Arbitrary Chow-power determinants use other bundle
types; they do not furnish that datum.

A proof or certificate may be accepted while an application remains conditional.
An exact reduction may be proved while its finite computation remains unexecuted.
A source-reviewed result need not have a promoted ID. Characteristic extensions
in the mathematical homes do not silently broaden older canonical records.

The [frontier](FRONTIER.md) gives concrete missing implications; the
[mechanism map](MAP.md) locates tools. Historical priorities and execution
chronology are not part of mathematical status.
