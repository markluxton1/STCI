# Mathematical status

For the fixed embedded rational quartic \(C_0\), unrestricted STCI existence
in characteristic zero remains **open**. The [result records](results/INDEX.md)
own promoted statements and exact hypotheses. The linked mathematical proof
homes also retain reviewed provisional results and conditional reductions;
consolidation alone does not promote them.

## Established boundaries

- [R-0001](results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) excludes every integral
  quartic carrier with any mate for this embedding in characteristic zero.
  Together with the lower-degree and factor arguments, both defining degrees
  of a hypothetical characteristic-zero STCI are at least five.
- [R-0022](results/quartic_44_filtered_exclusion.md) excludes a quartic pair
  independently. Its two mechanisms are actual filtered contact and the
  local-singularity/global-surface contradiction. Direct blowups give a
  classification-free presentation of the second, retaining a global bound.
- In every characteristic \(p>0\), the same fixed curve has an STCI
  presentation with a quartic carrier. [Explicit constructions and fixed-carrier
  degree restrictions](supplements/POSITIVE_CHARACTERISTIC_CONSTRUCTIONS.md)
  explain why the characteristic-zero exclusion cannot be transferred wholesale.
- The [full-conductor criterion](results/fixed_carrier_conductor_criterion.md)
  is a fixed-carrier equivalence. Necessary normalization models and structured
  phase reductions do not constitute computed existence or exclusion for all
  higher-degree carriers.

## Reviewed contributions with bounded scope

The current proof homes make the scope explicit: saturation and actual-source
conditions for [filtrations](synthesis/03-infinitesimal-geometry.md);
actual examples and family-dependent [residual bounds](synthesis/residual_intersections_and_jets.md);
[elliptic polarization and node-gluing failures](synthesis/projection_and_elliptic_gluing.md);
[partial marked absorption](synthesis/marked_deformation_and_absorption.md);
and [full contact/endpoint models](synthesis/contact_duality_and_global_descent.md).
They support constructive and obstructive investigations without resolving the
unrestricted question.

The [conditional quintic–octic theorem](synthesis/quintic_octic_surface_constraints.md)
excludes the subclass with the specified generic curvilinear algebra, reduced
full quintic first-symbol divisor and globally ADE quintic. Its integral Picard
and double-cover step goes beyond its rank count. The full \((5,8)\) problem
remains open.

A proof or certificate may be accepted while an application remains conditional.
An exact reduction may be proved while its finite computation remains unexecuted.
A source-reviewed result need not have a promoted ID. Characteristic extensions
in the mathematical homes do not silently broaden older canonical records.

The [frontier](FRONTIER.md) gives concrete missing implications; the
[mechanism map](MAP.md) locates tools. Historical priorities and execution
chronology are not part of mathematical status.
