# Mathematical status

For the fixed embedded rational quartic \(C_0\), unrestricted STCI existence
in characteristic zero remains **open**. The [result records](results/INDEX.md)
own promoted statements and exact hypotheses. The linked mathematical proof
homes also retain reviewed provisional results and conditional reductions;
consolidation alone does not promote them.

## Established boundaries

- [R-0001](results/quartic_carrier_exclusion.md) excludes every integral
  quartic carrier with any mate for this embedding in characteristic zero.
  Together with the lower-degree and factor arguments, both defining degrees
  of a hypothetical characteristic-zero STCI are at least five.
- [R-0022](results/quartic_44_filtered_exclusion.md) excludes a quartic pair
  independently. Its two mechanisms are actual filtered contact and the
  local-singularity/global-surface contradiction. Direct blowups give a
  classification-free presentation of the second, retaining a global bound.
- In every characteristic \(p>0\), the same fixed curve has an STCI
  presentation with a quartic carrier. [Explicit constructions and fixed-carrier
  degree restrictions](supplements/POSITIVE_CHARACTERISTIC_CONSTRUCTIONS.md)
  explain why the characteristic-zero exclusion cannot be transferred wholesale.
- The [full-conductor criterion](results/fixed_carrier_conductor_criterion.md)
  is a fixed-carrier equivalence. Necessary normalization models and structured
  phase reductions do not constitute computed existence or exclusion for all
  higher-degree carriers.

## Reviewed contributions with bounded scope

The current proof homes make the scope explicit: saturation and actual-source
conditions for [filtrations](synthesis/03-infinitesimal-geometry.md);
actual examples and family-dependent [residual bounds](synthesis/residual_intersections_and_jets.md);
[elliptic polarization and node-gluing failures](synthesis/projection_and_elliptic_gluing.md);
[partial marked absorption](synthesis/marked_deformation_and_absorption.md);
and [full contact/endpoint models](synthesis/contact_duality_and_global_descent.md).
They support constructive and obstructive investigations without resolving the
unrestricted question.

The [conditional quintic–octic theorem](synthesis/quintic_octic_surface_constraints.md)
excludes the subclass with the specified generic curvilinear algebra, reduced
full quintic first-symbol divisor and globally ADE quintic. Its integral Picard
and double-cover step goes beyond its rank count. The full \((5,8)\) problem
remains open.

The [reviewed sextic-pair reduction](supplements/SEXTIC_PAIR_REDUCTION.md)
covers every \((6,6)\) candidate in characteristic zero or \(p\ge7\):
it must be generically curvilinear with one of three nonzero defect profiles.
The \(\mathcal O(-7)\) row requires a second defect of degree two through
five, with exact apolar and actual-pole support restrictions. An explicit
local rank-nine complete intersection realizes a positive second defect,
without constructing global sextic equations.
In characteristic zero its pencil supplies normal integral sextics with an
actual Cartier-index-nine point, and its common first-normal divisor must be
nonreduced. The order-three class cover has exact canonical and Euler
invariants, imposing additional irregularity constraints. If a general
pencil carrier has only ADE singularities, the
[reviewed ADE reduction](supplements/SEXTIC_ADE_REDUCTION.md) leaves only
\(L=\mathcal O(-6),D_2=0\), with a primitive saturated triple in one
of three retained ambient torus orbits.
Their full sextic spaces now have dimension 27 in each orbit, with seven
directions beyond quartic-source multiples by quadrics. The first necessary
higher-contact determinant is explicit; the remaining contact equations
and proper sole support have not been decided.
That case and the non-ADE divisor/lift problem remain open; these
provisional deductions do not exclude the degree pair.

In positive characteristic, the [reviewed trace bound](supplements/EQUIVARIANT_DEGREE_BOUNDS.md)
gives \(p\mid ab\) when one defining equation is homogeneous for weights
\((0,1,3,4)\), with an arbitrary mate. The minimum larger degree in that
class is exactly \(p\) for \(p\ge7\). The same sharp minimum holds when
each equation has at most two weights. For arbitrary pairs with \(p\nmid ab\),
both extreme-component pairs have nonunit gcds in \(I_C\), and the full
tropical family has positive embedded movement; neither condition excludes
all such pairs. [Effective characteristic transfer](supplements/EFFECTIVE_CHARACTERISTIC_TRANSFER.md)
gives explicit prime thresholds for bounded-degree existence. Combined with
the promoted quartic exclusion and a monic-remainder coefficient reduction,
it gives the unconditional all-quartic mate-degree bound
\(\beta_4(p)\gg\sqrt{\log\log p/\log\log\log p}\).
These extensions are independently reviewed provisional deductions.
A growing unrestricted lower bound remains equivalent to characteristic-zero
nonexistence; the quantitative growth consequence retains that hypothesis.

The [mixed-characteristic trace extension](supplements/MIXED_CHARACTERISTIC_TRACE_OBSTRUCTION.md)
constrains arbitrary deformations of both equations from a special pair of
torus eigenforms. For a supported characteristic-zero generic fiber it gives
\(e\,v_p(ab)\ge2r\), where \(r\) is the least valuation of the
normalized coefficients breaking the symmetry. Thus \(v_p(ab)=1\)
excludes unramified good reduction at that special pair. With generic
curvilinearity and the stated strong support containment, it also excludes
a lift over \(W_2\). These reviewed provisional statements do not
exclude ramified traits or calculate the full horizontal incidence.

The [reviewed power-extraction theorem](supplements/PRIME_TO_P_POWER_EXTRACTION.md)
uses the full support condition: if the special pair is \((F_0,H_0^n)\),
the base pair is supported on \(C_0\), the first carrier is generically
smooth along it, and \(p\nmid n\), any actual supported DVR lift
extracts a degree-\(\deg H_0\) equation, modulo the first equation.
Applied to \((G_p,F_4^n)\), it excludes all ramified supported lifts
of these specified points by the characteristic-zero quartic theorem.
They lie on no horizontal component of their fixed-degree incidence.
For \(n=p^e n_0\), only the prime-to-\(p\) factor \(n_0\)
is removed; the pure \(p\)-power and arbitrary-presentation cases remain
open. This is not unrestricted degree growth.

For that same powered-quartic family, the
[reviewed Frobenius-power theorem](supplements/FROBENIUS_POWER_LIFTING_PRECISION.md)
gives exact maximum unramified strong precision \(1+v_p(n)\), allowing
both defining equations to vary. The naive integral forms attain the bound;
their next Artin level has strictly larger curve-ideal nilpotence index.
Any remaining supported characteristic-zero trait must satisfy
\(p\mid n\), \(\deg G\ge4p\), and \(v_\pi(p)\ge p-1\).
These are bounds on liftability within the specified family. No supported
trait at the remaining ramification indices or unrestricted degree bound
has been established.
The same proof home gives an all-ramification geometric restriction: the
flat closure of the generic multiplicity-\(p\) root must acquire a
nonzero embedded-point defect supported at one or both endpoints of \(C_0\).
Its absence would force a relative Cartier root and an ambient quartic
mate by Picard rigidity. The defect has an exact arithmetic-genus formula;
neither its impossibility nor a trait realizing it has been established.

A proof or certificate may be accepted while an application remains conditional.
An exact reduction may be proved while its finite computation remains unexecuted.
A source-reviewed result need not have a promoted ID. Characteristic extensions
in the mathematical homes do not silently broaden older canonical records.

The [frontier](FRONTIER.md) gives concrete missing implications; the
[mechanism map](MAP.md) locates tools. Historical priorities and execution
chronology are not part of mathematical status.
