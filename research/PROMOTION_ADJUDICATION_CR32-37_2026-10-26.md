# Adjudication: CR-32–37 — arithmetic ruled models
Date: 2026-10-26. Six claims verified against arithmetic note and independent audit, no fresh script execution and no canonical promotion.

- **CR-32 VERIFIED, fixed surface.** Over A=Z_(2), X:x1⁴=x0³x3 has finite normalization P_A(1,1,4) and ruled resolution F4. Normalization commutes with the specified fiber changes; not a general family theorem.
- **CR-33 VERIFIED.** Full homogeneous conductor is (x0,x1)², including the vertex; on F4 its extension is O(-2E-6fInfinity).
- **CR-34 VERIFIED.** D_M:q⁴-(st³)⁴ is relative effective Cartier, finite flat of degree four over P1_A with module O⊕O(-4)⊕O(-8)⊕O(-12). Projective CI flatness is a separate assertion (R-0021).
- **CR-35 VERIFIED.** In characteristic zero the four sections meet pairwise in 3p0+pInfinity on F4. In characteristic two the special fiber is a fourth Cartier multiple of one section.
- **CR-36 VERIFIED, base-change qualification.** The mu4 orbit map has scheme image D_M, but restricting to a zero of st³ yields reduced endpoint orbit image, not the length-four fiber of D_M. Scheme image does not commute with this restriction.
- **CR-37 VERIFIED over B=A[i].** Pairwise root-labeled section intersections have horizontal 3p0+pInfinity plus vertical coefficient one for adjacent roots, two for opposite roots (v_(1-i)(2)=2). Root labels unavailable over A.

Sources: notes/2026-10-24-ruled-surfaces-arithmetic.md and notes/2026-10-24-four-sheet-arithmetic-independent-audit.md.

Disposition: one scoped arithmetic ruled-model package, cross-referencing R-0021; do not generalize to arbitrary characteristic-two lifting.
