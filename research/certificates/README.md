# Exact proof support

The [computation guide](../computations/README.md) gives the mathematical
entry points, checking commands and essential input families. Exact source
programs and witnesses remain at the paths consumed by their checkers;
this directory does not duplicate them.

Start from the [canonical result](../results/INDEX.md) or focused proof to
learn what a certificate establishes. The [fixed-sextic result](../results/ci_fixed_sextic_jet_rigidity.md)
explains why its sampled matrix proves the global constant-coefficient
kernel. The [degree15 dual account](../supplements/LOCAL_COHOMOLOGY_DEGREE15_CERTIFICATES.md)
explains its complete polynomial tensor identity. The
[fixed-boundary smoothing proof](../synthesis/fixed_boundary_smoothing.md)
connects exact tangent witnesses to formal and étale-local node parameters.
These are different mathematical arguments, not interchangeable PASS records.

Retain the smallest adequate exact support together with its generators,
parameters, characteristic, source space and geometric interpretation.
Saved rank alone does not supply the source-identification or coverage proof.
A checker that can reconstruct a witness is not evidence that it completed.
See the [mathematical artifact policy](../ARTIFACT_POLICY.md).
