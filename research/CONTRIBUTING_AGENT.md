# STCI research contribution

## Exact statement or question

State the curve, characteristic, hypotheses, quantifiers, and conclusion. Keep
conditional assertions, finite reductions, actual constructions, and open
questions distinct. A Result states its mathematics in its own document.

## Proof routes and evidence

Give the actual proof or an exact Argument section containing it. Keep
alternative routes and their joint prerequisites distinct. Explain the
invariant assertion and coverage of a necessary computation and retain its
smallest exact support. Exploratory calculations belong to their Investigation.

## Scoped independent review

A reviewer reconstructs the reasoning and tests omitted cases, hidden
assumptions, counterexamples, dependency scope, and characteristic restrictions.
Distinguish generic statements from all-point assertions and formal/local
existence from global equations. Record accepted, pending, or disputed review
on the precise proof route, with what was read or executed and qualifications.
A review decision is the mathematical assessment; there is no second Result
classification or acceptance ceremony.

## Effect on research

State what follows, what remains open, and which Framework or Investigation is
affected. A completed activity or a successful computation does not answer a
broader question without the missing mathematical argument. Use [MODEL.md](MODEL.md)
for metadata and [the evidence rules](AUTHORITY.md) for review and retention.
