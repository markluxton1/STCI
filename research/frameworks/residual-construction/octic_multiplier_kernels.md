# The octic construction beyond first compatibility

## Octic multiplier kernels and common-zero divisors

Apply the [actual octic quotient](../../arguments/spectral/actual_octic_root_lift_comparison.md#application-to-the-24-first-compatible-order-three-roots)
to the 24 known first-compatible roots, whose sextic tests are complete.
Positive kernel-dimension bounds do not prove joint generation;
exact kernels, fixed divisors, and proper pairs require separate checks. The existing perturbation
argument gives generic length eight and residual degree 32 when joint generation holds. Higher contact is a separate
question, and sole support for (8,8) would require length sixteen.
