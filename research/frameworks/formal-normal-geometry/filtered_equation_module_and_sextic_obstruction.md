## A sextic source image and a distinct cubic obstruction

For the conditional movement-two (6,16) lane, the degree-six quadratic-symbol map has source dimension 23, kernel generated by the containing quadric cubed, rank 22 and target dimension 33. Its eleven independent image equations constrain the product of the moving root and its multiplier. A specified smooth torsion-two seed satisfies all eleven; for its selected sextic representative the next cubic comparison has the exact pairing \(-384\), and the seed fails the separate embedded second obstruction. Another sextic with the same quadratic symbol differs by eta*q³. The exact row annihilates this additional cubic direction, so the nonzero pairing excludes all sextic lifts of this specified seed. See [the complete all-lifts calculation](../../arguments/carriers/normalization_conductor_phase2_addendum.md#explicit-torsion-two-seed-failing-the-cubic-lift).

For a nonzero quadratic root \(f=au^2+buv+cv^2\), cubic compatibility has the undivided equations \(h_0=a\ell\), \(h_1=b\ell+am\), \(h_2=c\ell+bm\), \(h_3=cm\), where \(\deg\ell,\deg m\le7\). Multiplication by \(f\) is injective, with a sixteen-dimensional correction image in forty cubic coefficients and a twenty-four-dimensional quotient. Singular roots, multiplier zeros, the twenty-five second-obstruction pairings and full-root torsion remain separate constraints. See [the explicit source and coefficient calculation](../../arguments/carriers/normalization_conductor_phase2_addendum.md#coefficient-level-cubic-obstruction-without-elimination).

For the specified seed, the kernel-direction check upgrades the cubic failure to an exclusion of every sextic lift with that symbol. The independently established second ambient obstruction excludes that root under its own bridge hypotheses. Neither test excludes the full (6,16) incidence.
