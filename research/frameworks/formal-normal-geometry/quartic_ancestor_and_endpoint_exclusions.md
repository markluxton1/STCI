## Source-preserved quartic-ancestor conormal and endpoint restrictions

These statements concern **quartic inverse-system ancestors**, not arbitrary STCI pairs or arbitrary-degree mates. Their proofs depend on the finite-flat quadruple, socle-pencil and complete quartic-fiber antecedents cited in the original notes.

The [intrinsic conormal splitting criterion](../../arguments/local-cohomology/local_cohomology_conormal_geometry.md) constructs V from the canonical triple ideal J by taking the torsion-free quotient of J/(I_C J). Its intrinsic extension has a line subbundle L cubed, twisted by the second defect, and a complementary conormal quotient. The actual two quartic targets map V surjectively onto the socle line O(-14), since their images form a basepoint-free pencil. The kernel has degree 2e-14, and the extension splits because H1(O(2e)) vanishes for e>=0. Therefore a common quartic ancestor necessarily has V=O(2e-14) direct sum O(-14). The criterion is necessary, not sufficient. For e=1, the remaining d2=1,2,3,4 strata require respective Hankel connecting-matrix ranks 3,2,1,0; in particular the d2=4 zero-extension/Gorenstein case remains possible at this stage. These are not STCI BF bounds.

The [corrected simultaneous-target bridge](../../arguments/carriers/ramification_and_socle_controls.md#source-2026-10-07-session-socle-bridge-adversarial-auditmd) excludes primitive e=1,d2=0 common quartic ancestors under the accepted finite-flat and socle antecedents, using both target equations together. The actual image of a multiplier in the quadruple is a nonzero socle target; it must not be set to zero. The valid local equation retains that target, and elimination between the two multipliers yields the contradiction. The older single-multiplier pole argument is invalid for ancestors.

The [six defective e=1,d2=1 endpoints](../../arguments/local-cohomology/ancestor_and_conductor_endpoints.md#source-2026-10-08-session-localcoh-e1-d1-endpoint-ancestorsmd) are excluded as *ancestors*: four have a one-dimensional complete ambient quartic space, incompatible with two independent target images; two have a common cubic factor and reduce to degree-one multipliers, excluded by P-037. This does not rule out an arbitrary higher-degree mate for a remaining unique quartic carrier. Likewise the [principal open-chart rank result](../../arguments/carriers/principal_mf6_normalization.md#source-2026-10-08-session-mf6-principal-rank-open-independent-auditmd) gives dimension one for the complete quartic space on its specified open, not an all-mate carrier exclusion; boundary charts require their separate proofs.

The remaining defective ancestor strata and other directions must be checked with their actual extension classes and target images. None of these conditional exclusions can imply a universal STCI theorem by dropping their antecedents.


## Two additional October 8 ancestor exclusions

**Quadric first-quotient direction.** For the fixed rational quartic C of class (1,3) on the smooth quadric Q, the conormal direction A=-z/2, B=1 is precisely the direction inside Q. Its canonical double is 2C on Q. Any containing quartic F restricts to a section of O_Q(4,4)(-2C)=O_Q(2,-2), which has no sections. Thus both quartic ancestor equations would be divisible by Q, reducing to the prohibited common quadratic ancestor P-037. This excludes that **direction for every second-defect divisor**, conditional on the canonical-triple reduction and P-037; it does not exclude every positive-defect or Gorenstein stratum. [Source and complete 35-monomial rank check](../../arguments/local-cohomology/ancestor_and_conductor_endpoints.md#source-2026-10-08-session-localcoh-quadric-directionmd).

**Two algebraic defect-four directions.** For A=r*z, B=1 with 12r²+4r+3=0 and second-defect degree four, a genuine corrected triple has relation delta*m+theta*y²=0 with theta a nonzero constant, normalized to one. The complete seven-dimensional ambient double-quartic space has two primitive members and five coefficients (h,E)=(a_i*z^(i+2),z^i), i=0,...,4, with pairwise distinct a_i. Quartic containment is h=delta*E. Eliminating the two primitive coefficients leaves a seven-by-five matrix. For delta=d0+...+d4*z^4, its low and high minors are d0^4 and d4^4; when both vanish, the next minors are d1^5 and d3^5. For the remaining delta=d2*z², a diagonal minor has entries d2-a_i, at most one zero. Thus the kernel always has dimension at most one: no two independent quartics contain the triple. This excludes common quartic ancestors for **these two directions and every defect section**, not all defect-four directions or arbitrary-degree mates. [Exact source](../../arguments/local-cohomology/ancestor_and_conductor_endpoints.md#source-2026-10-08-session-localcoh-e1-d4-algebraicmd).

## P-044 dx=1 slice: exact five-coordinate saturation (October 7)

The [dx=1 saturation audit](https://github.com/markluxton1/STCI/blob/c68a8cb1f3ade682172bce338abd632f79cf0881/research/supplements/OCT7_SATURATION_REES_AND_CARRIER_CLASSIFICATION.md#2026-10-07-dx1-saturation-audit) proves a **slice-level** classification for P-044's normalized primitive-fourth obstruction, not a universal STCI theorem. Write `A=z²+az+x`, `B=1+bz+dz²`, impose `d=1/x`, and put `u=bx-a`, `t=xu`, `alpha=2a+1`, `beta=b+2`. The basepoint-free slice is precisely `D(t)`. Reconstructing all five original obstruction coordinates gives an ideal `L=(Q1,...,Q5)` whose saturation is the six-generator ideal

`L:t^infinity = J = (x beta²+alpha beta-2alpha², (4x+2)alpha beta-3alpha², beta³, alpha beta², alpha² beta, alpha³)`.

The exact certificates are `tJ subset L subset J` and `J:t=J`. The latter also follows from the free rank-four `Q[x]` quotient `R/J` with basis `1,alpha,beta,gamma`, `gamma=beta²-(8/3)alpha beta`: multiplication by t has determinant `[x(1/2-2x)]^4`, hence is injective. The radical is `(alpha,beta)`, so the basepoint-free zero locus is `a=-1/2`, `b=-2`, `x!=0,1/4`. The scheme is **nonreduced**; its underlying support alone does not preserve the four-dimensional quotient structure. The formerly proposed three-coordinate saturation is false: all five coordinates are necessary. The note checks agreement with P-045 on this slice, without claiming that the slice exhausts P-044's full parameter space. Preserve this exact ideal as a distinct boundary calculation rather than treating its previous audit gap as current.


## Primitive e=1 triples versus defective d2=4 ancestor directions

The [October 7 primitive-triple audit](../../arguments/normal-geometry/mf6_and_nonnormal_structures.md#multiplicity-six-models-and-nonnormal-structures) distinguishes a stronger quartic-carrier statement from the weaker quartic-ancestor rank tests. In characteristic zero, for first quotient `O(-6)` and `D2=0`, the basepoint-free primitive directions comprise one principal torus orbit and three boundary orbits. The principal and two algebraic boundary orbits have complete two-dimensional quartic pencils with forced ruling-line base components; the comparison-zero boundary has quartics divisible by the unique quadric. The fixed-line observation alone does **not** exclude arbitrary mates, since a line can meet C at its forced intersection points. The subsequent normalizations and full inverse-support analysis in the independent audit exclude the pure quartic carriers for mates of any positive degree under the stated MF6 hypotheses. Do not preserve the earlier fixed-line-only argument as a complete all-degree proof. The defective `D2>0` cases remain outside this primitive result.

By contrast, the [October 8 algebraic d2=4 audit](../../arguments/local-cohomology/ancestor_and_conductor_endpoints.md#source-2026-10-08-session-localcoh-e1-d4-independent-auditmd) is a **common-quartic-ancestor** exclusion for precisely the two directions `A=r z, B=1`, `12r²+4r+3=0`, uniform in every genuine quartic defect section. The correction coefficient theta must be nonzero: theta zero would produce torsion `(R/(delta))*m` at a zero of delta and violate finite flatness. Normalizing theta=1 gives `y²=-delta*m` and quartic containment `h=delta E`. The complete quartic source has dimension seven; a seven-by-five matrix has rank at least four for every delta, including repeated and infinity zeros, leaving at most one containing quartic. Two independent target classes are therefore impossible **conditional on the canonical-triple ancestor reduction**. This does not exclude all d2=4 directions or arbitrary-degree mates.


## Six degree-one defective endpoints: fixed-carrier exclusion stronger than ancestor rank

The [October 7 endpoint audit](../../arguments/normal-geometry/mf6_and_nonnormal_structures.md#multiplicity-six-models-and-nonnormal-structures) treats all six `e=1,d2=1` boundary directions (`a0=0` or `b1=0`), **not** the principal `a0*b1!=0` locus or the `d2=2` stratum. For each endpoint the complete ambient quartic space is computed from all 35 monomials. At the rational root `r=-1/2`, the quartics are a fixed cubic times linear forms; at either algebraic root of `12r²+4r+3=0`, the space is one-dimensional and generated by an explicit irreducible quartic. The latter has third-layer defect `d3=4`, incompatible with MF6 `(d2,d3)=(1,3)`, but that numerical contradiction alone is not an arbitrary-mate exclusion.

The stronger fixed-carrier result uses an explicit dominant birational parametrization of an affine chart of each algebraic quartic by `k[u,v,(u+kappa)^(-1)]`, where `3kappa²-2kappa+1=0`, with full inverse support of C equal to the diagonal `v=u`. A hypothetical mate pullback must be `c(v-u)^n(u+kappa)^k`. Comparing the two branches over the singular line forces `k=-n` and `(-kappa)^n=1`; conjugation and `Norm(kappa)=1/3` contradict `n>0`. Coordinate reversal and torus symmetry transfer the argument to the other endpoints. This proves an **all-degree mate exclusion for these six fixed endpoint carrier directions**. The open principal `d2=1` locus and the `d2=2` stratum are not covered by this scoped argument. This strengthens, without contradicting, the earlier ancestor-only quartic-space statement above; the general R-0001 theorem subsequently excludes all integral quartic carriers by a different global route.


## Defective e=1 MF6 structural conditions

The [October 7 defective structural note](../../arguments/normal-geometry/mf6_and_nonnormal_structures.md#multiplicity-six-models-and-nonnormal-structures) gives a local Hilbert–Burch presentation for a positive-second-defect canonical triple: `J=(delta*m-gamma*y^2,m*y,m^2,y^3)`, with gamma invertible at zeros of delta. Its torsion-free generator bundle has extension `0 -> L^3(D2) -> V -> M(-D2) -> 0`, where `L=O(-6), M=O(-8)`. The `-D2` twist in the quotient is essential. The normalized cubic residue uses `delta^2*f3`, yielding a class in `H1(O(-10+2*d2))` that must vanish after mapping to `H1(O(-10+d2+d3))`.

For `(d2,d3)=(1,3)` the necessary condition is a nonzero kernel of the five-by-three Hankel matrix on seven Laurent coefficients (all ten maximal minors vanish). For `(2,2)` the class in `H1(O(-6))` must vanish in all five coordinates. These conditions are not sufficient for a quartic carrier or mate. The first quadratic-cocycle killing test gives a sextic direction locus `c1*c3-c2^2=0` for `d2=1`; for `d2=2`, a defect quadratic `delta0+delta1*z+delta2*z^2` must satisfy `delta0*c1+delta1*c2+delta2*c3=0`. In each case finite flatness imposes an additional no-common-zero condition on delta and the corrected coefficient gamma. Primitive obstruction-zero directions cannot acquire a genuine positive defect of degree at most three by multiplying their unique correction by delta.

The [primitive progress note](../../arguments/normal-geometry/mf6_and_nonnormal_structures.md#multiplicity-six-models-and-nonnormal-structures) records the exact `H1(O(-4))` quadratic cocycle, three Laurent obstruction coordinates, and classification into one principal and three boundary torus orbits. Its incomplete conjugate-orbit checkpoint was superseded by the later [independent audit](../../arguments/normal-geometry/mf6_and_nonnormal_structures.md#multiplicity-six-models-and-nonnormal-structures). The six degree-one boundary directions have the stronger all-degree exclusions recorded above. The principal `d2=1` sextic and `(2,2)` conditions remain open within this structural method, without contradicting the separate global quartic-carrier exclusion R-0001.
