# 3. From common normal factors to actual equations

This chapter explains **four different levels of structure**: generic normal forms, global graded relations, embedded infinitesimal lifts, and actual complete intersections. Moving from one level to the next requires additional hypotheses. See [Chapter 2](../residual-construction/two_surfaces.md) for the symmetric geometry of the original equations.

**Authority boundary.** The conormal/socle budget [R-0032](../../results/full_conormal_socle_budget.md) is necessary for actual supported complete intersections, not sufficient to produce an embedding or equations. For the scope of the local comparison [R-0030](../../results/local_filtration_comparison_defect.md), see the [open sole-support question](open_normal_geometry.md#punctual-relative-defect-under-sole-support). The October 27 embedded one-step criterion is reviewed source-level work.

**Varying-lift qualification.** A fixed-lift cubic failure need not exclude all sextic lifts of the same quadratic symbol: the free \(\eta q^3\) direction must also be tested. The [focused sextic-lifting discussion](characteristic_two_and_sextic_lifts.md#310c-a-failed-seed-and-an-exact-cubic-correction) records the augmented correction map, the exact \(-384\) obstruction for the specified seed, and its limited scope. The [full coefficient calculation](../../arguments/carriers/normalization_conductor_phase2_addendum.md#explicit-torsion-two-seed-failing-the-cubic-lift) remains the proof source.


The [global equation-jet filtration](../../arguments/equations/global_equation_jets.md) makes the source
images intrinsic to the actual embedding and gives finite realization results.
The [spectral power construction](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_pushforward_powers.md) and
[determinant schemes](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_determinant_schemes.md) produce graded families
under their stated hypotheses. The [torsion-two lifting calculation](../../arguments/spectral/quadratic_root_torsion_two_lifting.md)
then tests an actual ambient operator on specified strata. These are different
steps; a graded family does not supply its homogeneous equation lifts.
