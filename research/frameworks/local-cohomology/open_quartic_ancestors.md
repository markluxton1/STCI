# The two-target quartic ancestor problem

## Quartic common ancestors in local cohomology

For the fixed \(C_0\) over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field,
can actual quartics \(F,G\in I_4\) and a homogeneous \(\alpha\in M_{-7}\)
satisfy \(F\alpha=u\), \(G\alpha=v\), where \(M=H_I^2(S)\), \(I=I_{C_0}\),
\(S=k[x,y,z,w]\), \(u=xz/(qB)\), and \(v=yw/(qB)\), with \(q=xw-yz\)
and \(B=xz^2-y^2w\)? The [finite order-four reduction](../../arguments/local-cohomology/direction_and_apolarity.md#uniform-finite-reduction-for-the-remaining-degree-four-locus)
puts \(\alpha\) in the 30-dimensional space \((0:_M I^4)_{-7}\), with
principal-part order exactly four. Each quartic source is 18-dimensional.
The [finite moment and tensor proof](../../results/quartic_ancestor_direction_reduction.md)
now excludes all primitive directions of degrees zero and two, and every
degree-one direction with a component zero at either toric endpoint.
It includes every admissible top coefficient and lower correction in
those strata. Its necessary quadratic moment is
\(\mu_2((rU-pV)^2)=0\).

The remaining normal-dual directions normalize to
\[
p=1+t,\qquad r=b+ct,\qquad bc(b-c)\ne0,
\]
on the line \(b=2\) or the degree-twelve curve
\(P_{12}(b,c)=0\). The [exact determinant and remaining interface](../../results/quartic_ancestor_direction_reduction.md#remaining-interface)
define \(P_{12}\) without a coefficient dump.
Retain the full top-and-quadratic kernel in
\((h_0,\ldots,h_4,\ell)\), both actual lower multiplication equations
in the full 74-row tensor, and ambient coprimality.
The existing canonical-triple reductions leave \(d_2=2,3,4\) and
\(d_3=4\) in this degree-one lane.

The [ruling-ratio slices](../../arguments/local-cohomology/direction_and_apolarity.md#new-all-stage-exclusion-both-ruling-ratio-slices),
[explicit all-stage family](../../arguments/local-cohomology/local_cohomology_ancestor_obstructions.md#3-an-integral-coefficient-functional-excludes-the-whole-explicit-family),
[normalized parity certificate](../../arguments/local-cohomology/contact_parity_and_mf6.md#a-corrected-polynomial-dual-exclusion-for-the-normalized-parity-family)
and [complete rational endpoint certificates](../../arguments/local-cohomology/degree_fifteen_obstruction.md#complete-rational-endpoint-ancestor-exclusions)
retain their separate exact proofs and provide controls.
Non-quasi-cyclicity of the full module needs an argument beyond this fixed
degree. The quartic-mate theorem concerns sole support, whereas the ancestor
[generic length calculation](../../arguments/local-cohomology/direction_and_apolarity.md#2026-10-05-localcoh-generic-length)
gives a degree-four residual cycle for any such pair.
