# Spectral roots, Picard torsion, and embedded realization

## Scope

For a degree-two map \(\pi:\Sigma\to\mathbf P^1\) from a smooth genus-one curve, let \(A=\pi^*\mathcal O(1)\), let \(B\) be a degree-two line bundle, and put \(\eta=B\otimes A^{-1}\). Torsion of \(\eta\) controls the splitting of \(\pi_*B^n\), coefficient determinants, and graded root constructions. The [all-power pushforward theorem](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_pushforward_powers.md) and [determinant-scheme continuation](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_determinant_schemes.md) establish that spectral mechanism on their stated smooth-root loci. It is a mechanism for graded data; the fixed embedding's actual equation maps impose additional constraints.

In particular, a spectral trivialization does not automatically yield an actual homogeneous map or a proper pair in \(\mathbf P^3\). The [global equation-jet account](../../arguments/equations/global_equation_jets.md) retains those source and lifting maps. The [global equation realization framework](../global-equation-realization/index.md) states the final support requirement; this entry isolates spectral input and where its reach stops.

## Available mathematics and controls

- The genus-one pushforward splitting detects \(\eta^n\cong\mathcal O_\Sigma\) and produces graded relations. Singular and nonreduced roots require the separate boundary controls in [spectral pushforward boundary](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_pushforward_boundary.md); conclusions on smooth roots cannot silently be extended to them.
- In the conditional characteristic-zero \((6,16)\) lane, the hypotheses include an actual supported pair, generic coprime normal complete intersection \((2,12)\), and an actual saturated movement-two first relation. They force \(\mathcal O_Z(1,-1)^{12}\cong\mathcal O_Z\) on the \((2,2)\) root. The reviewed finite singular-root bridge supplies embedded Cartier lifts, but the actual sextic must still satisfy cubic-jet matching in the image of its global source map; the 23-dimensional sextic source has rank-22 symbol image with kernel generated by \(q^3\). See the [conditional phase-two interface](../../arguments/carriers/normalization_conductor_phase2_addendum.md#conditional-616-singular-root-interface) and [rank-stratified lifting account](../../arguments/spectral/rank_strata_and_sextic_lifts.md). These are conditional constraints, not an unconditional exclusion or construction.
- The [order-three root and sextic-lifting study](../../arguments/spectral/order_three_roots_and_sextic_lifts.md) parameterizes a specified smooth-root locus and tests a specified four-parameter embedded slice. Its coverage does not include every root or arbitrary equation pair.
- The [actual octic/root comparison](../../arguments/spectral/actual_octic_root_lift_comparison.md) exhibits a proper degree-eight pair with transverse length eight and residual degree 32. This is positive realization with residual support, not a sole-support construction.
- The sole-support \((6,6)\) problem is a separate lane: spectral torsion arguments tied to a movement-two first relation do not classify those pairs. The [residual-construction framework](../residual-construction/index.md) is relevant where the mechanism constructs or transfers residual support; its conclusions do not collapse the spectral and \((6,6)\) questions.

## Bridges

The main gap is between Picard torsion/graded splitting and the image of the actual embedded equation operator. A bridge must pass from a root trivialization through the specified source quotient, match the required higher jets (including multiplier zeros and node gluing when present), and certify that the resulting equations have the intended full intersection. A determinant vanishing or a kernel vector is only the spectral input.

Use [open root lifts](open_root_lifts.md) for the root-to-actual-equation interface. Compare with the [global equation-realization framework](../global-equation-realization/index.md), the [formal-normal framework](../formal-normal-geometry/index.md), and the [carrier-normalization framework](../carrier-normalization/index.md): local compatibility, normalized descent, and actual global sections are linked questions with distinct hypotheses.
