# Spectral roots, Picard torsion, and embedded realization

## Scope

For a degree-two map \(\pi:\Sigma\to\mathbf P^1\) from a smooth genus-one curve, let \(A=\pi^*\mathcal O(1)\), let \(B\) be a degree-two line bundle, and put \(\eta=B\otimes A^{-1}\). Torsion of \(\eta\) controls the splitting of \(\pi_*B^n\), coefficient determinants, and graded root constructions. The [all-power pushforward theorem](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_pushforward_powers.md) and [determinant-scheme continuation](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_determinant_schemes.md) establish that spectral mechanism on their stated smooth-root loci. It is a mechanism for graded data; the fixed embedding's actual equation maps impose additional constraints.

The [global equation-jet account](../../arguments/equations/global_equation_jets.md)
records the additional source and lifting maps needed for homogeneous
realization and properness.

## Available mathematics and controls

- The genus-one pushforward splitting detects \(\eta^n\cong\mathcal O_\Sigma\) and produces graded relations. Singular and nonreduced roots require the separate boundary controls in [spectral pushforward boundary](../../arguments/spectral/spectral_pushforward_boundary.md); conclusions on smooth roots cannot silently be extended to them.
- In the conditional characteristic-zero \((6,16)\) lane, the hypotheses include an actual supported pair, generic coprime normal complete intersection \((2,12)\), and an actual saturated movement-two first relation. They force \(\mathcal O_Z(1,-1)^{12}\cong\mathcal O_Z\) on the \((2,2)\) root. The reviewed finite singular-root bridge supplies embedded Cartier lifts, but the actual sextic must still satisfy cubic-jet matching in the image of its global source map; the 23-dimensional sextic source has rank-22 symbol image with kernel generated by \(q^3\). See the [conditional phase-two interface](../../arguments/carriers/normalization_conductor_phase2_addendum.md#conditional-616-singular-root-interface) and [rank-stratified lifting account](../../arguments/spectral/rank_strata_and_sextic_lifts.md). These are conditional constraints, not an unconditional exclusion or construction.
- The [order-three root and sextic-lifting study](../../arguments/spectral/order_three_roots_and_sextic_lifts.md) parameterizes a specified smooth-root locus and tests a specified four-parameter embedded slice. Its coverage does not include every root or arbitrary equation pair.
- The [actual octic/root comparison](../../arguments/spectral/actual_octic_root_lift_comparison.md) exhibits a proper degree-eight pair with transverse length eight and residual degree 32. This is positive realization with residual support, not a sole-support construction.
- The sole-support \((6,6)\) problem is a separate lane: spectral torsion arguments tied to a movement-two first relation do not classify those pairs. The [residual-construction framework](../residual-construction/index.md) is relevant where the mechanism constructs or transfers residual support; its conclusions do not collapse the spectral and \((6,6)\) questions.

## Bridges

The main gap is between Picard torsion/graded splitting and the image of the actual embedded equation operator. A bridge must pass from a root trivialization through the specified source quotient, match the required higher jets (including multiplier zeros and node gluing when present), and certify that the resulting equations have the intended full intersection. A determinant vanishing or a kernel vector is only the spectral input.

The [open root-lift problem](open_root_lifts.md) specifies the remaining
source conditions. [Global equation realization](../global-equation-realization/index.md),
[formal normal geometry](../formal-normal-geometry/index.md), and
[carrier normalization](../carrier-normalization/index.md) impose distinct
conditions on sections, local compatibility, and descent.

When a root construction produces an actual proper pair, its [full-primary attachment quotient](../../arguments/local-cohomology/actual_pair_coregularity.md#actual-attachment-equation-modules-are-the-dual-annihilator-layers) measures the residual by local duality irrespective of generic embedding dimension. The [rank-one layer formula](../../arguments/residuals/layerwise_attachment_and_triples.md#layerwise-attachment-duality-on-a-non-gorenstein-primary-component) requires a curvilinear primary, not an arbitrary higher-rank spectral root. Picard torsion is not a substitute for either module.

### The actual elliptic conormal pencil as a spectral input

In characteristic zero, the [actual elliptic comparison](../../arguments/carriers/elliptic_contact_and_spectral_comparison.md)
starts with divided normal coefficients, rather than a freely chosen
root. If \(g=\operatorname{length}(Z\cap E)\), the direction bundle is
\(B=A^3(-G)\), the horizontal root has type \((6-g,2)\), and its
normalization conductor has degree \(4-g\) on the base. Thus all
contact lengths \(0\le g\le4\) have an exact Picard comparison.
For \(g<4\), pullback to the elliptic normalization loses the root's
conductor gluing. At \(g=0\) that pullback is even trivial in the
actual failed member, while extra inverse support persists.

If a hypothetical octic mate also has its **actual** first degree-two
kernel as a line subbundle, ordinary normal flatness and the socle
formula force \(g(b-2)=24\). The four necessary rows
\((g,b)=(1,26),(2,14),(3,10),(4,8)\) retain full-root torsion and
actual-source conditions; nonsaturation remains unexcluded. The full
family's support question includes every contact stratum. Its fixed
marking prevents target sign symmetry, so sign-invariant ACM vanishing
does not directly answer it.

The [conjugate-source comparison](../../arguments/carriers/elliptic_conjugate_sources.md)
uses the disjoint quadratic residue fields to obtain an actual
\((F_8,\tau F_8)\) pair of generic normal type \((2,2)\) and residual
degree \(48\). In the saturated sector, the first cancellation has
exact poles on the vertical divisor \(V\); it is not a homogeneous
row operation. The equation-comparison defect then vanishes while
the full normal-layer degree defect is \(3b(b-6)\), so those two
defects cannot be substituted for each other.

For the separate spectral line of translated **cycles**, the
[marked-boundary norm comparison](../../arguments/torus/spectral_boundary_modulus.md#2-an-actual-carrier-relation-supplies-an-ordinary-norm)
constructs an ordinary product of norms for every actual
translated-support pair, including nonnormal and entirely-thick
carriers after row replacement and factorization. Its aggregate
first jet is already \(-4M_1=-2\Theta(F,G)\); the remaining task
is a geometric cancellation constraint, not another scalar evaluation.
That assertion remains distinct from root Picard torsion and from
homogeneous realization.
