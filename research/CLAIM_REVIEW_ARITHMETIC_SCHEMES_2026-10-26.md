## Dependencies and potential overclaims

- CR-32 and CR-39 are deliberately opposite specialization conclusions for different objects, not a contradiction.
- CR-34 and CR-36 distinguish flat scheme image from the orbit of a fixed endpoint.
- CR-35 and CR-38 distinguish pairwise intersections upstairs and downstairs; neither is a universal residual-contact bound.
- CR-44 and CR-45 require global algebra evidence beyond generic cusp/node geometry CR-46.
- CR-41 is a finite flat correspondence, not a group-scheme action by bitangent multipliers.
- CR-42 refines CR-16, not a second independent theorem.
- None of these geometric/arithmetic claims extends R-0021 to a universal characteristic-two lifting statement.
