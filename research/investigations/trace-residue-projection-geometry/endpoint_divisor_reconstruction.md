# Endpoint divisors as prescribed differential sections

Exploratory synthesis; governing problem is calculation of the first moment on the full translated-cycle CI realization locus. No universal vanishing asserted. The user's viewpoint: build the necessary global equation data from the differential sections and their endpoint divisors, in parallel with the earlier toric-limit concentration of zeros. Do not start by classifying carriers or sole support.

Classical inputs are CI adjunction, finite duality for the reduced immersion, and the normalization description of the dualizing sheaf. The existing smooth-support calculation is [the six ambient minors](../../arguments/torus/contact_jacobians_and_hamiltonian_actions.md#the-six-ambient-minors-different-and-contact-identity); the componentwise mixed-minor formula is [the spectral Jacobian bridge](../../arguments/torus/spectral_jacobian_first_jet_bridge.md). See [centered differential wedge](centered_differential_wedge.md).

For Y=V(F_a,G_b), D=Y_red, put Sigma=Ann(I_(D/Y)). Adjunction and finite duality identify Sigma(a+b-4)=omega_D and hence Sigma(a+b-2)=omega_D(2). All six Jacobian minors are sections of this same module. The reduced arrangement may be singular and omega_D need not be invertible. On the normalization the six specific sections are ordinary twisted regular differentials in omega_P1 tensor O_P1(8)=O_P1(6). Fix the canonical duality normalization by identifying D12 with n_i dw on the branch in z=1, as in the finite relative trace formula. Using r=t/s, the tuple on C_(u_i,v_i), after the common factor n_i, is
\[
(uvt^6,-3vs^2t^4,2us^3t^3,4vs^3t^3,-3us^4t^2,s^6).
\]
This also follows by complementing the tangent Plucker coordinates of the homogeneous parametrization. Endpoint divisor pairs (ord_0,ord_infty) are respectively (6,0),(4,2),(3,3),(3,3),(2,4),(0,6). The quotient of 2D03 by D12 on each normalized socle line is q=u/v. Divisor equality gives the ratio constant on each branch, not necessarily the same across branches.

In particular D03 and D12 are in the same two-dimensional space of distinguished sections (possibly dependent). Dividing by zw, whose divisor on each normalization is 4p0+4p_infty, leaves differentials with simple poles at the endpoints. Their branch residues at zero are 2 n_i q_i and 4 n_i, respectively. The centered combination (D03-cD12)/(zw) is regular in omega_D precisely when c=M_1/(2m), by residue cancellation on the shared points. This is exactly the earlier centered wedge congruence, not a new balancing condition. Regularity here is in the dualizing sheaf of the singular reduced arrangement, not in the ordinary canonical bundle of the normalization. If the branch ratios all agree, the centered combination is zero.

Research method: retain the endpoint divisor patterns and constants for every component, the common socle/dualizing module into which all six sections must fit, and their common origin from dF wedge dG for two homogeneous polynomials. Examine simultaneous divisibility and gluing of these prescribed sections, and actual polynomial syzygies and integrability of the common source where needed. The individual branch divisor prescriptions and the centered residue relation are already determined by the cycle and do not alone establish realizability or M_1=0. The remaining task is to determine how common source origin constrains the simultaneous section data, in particular whether the geometrically marked D03 itself can be divided by zw in the actual CI ring. Preserve the distinction between a necessary condition and a sufficient reconstruction of F,G.
