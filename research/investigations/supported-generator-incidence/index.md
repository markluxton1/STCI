# Two-equation incidence on bounded supported attracting families

## Scope

The initial target is the fixed embedded quartic over an algebraically closed
field of characteristic zero, type \((6,6)\), and polynomial \(36d-144\).
For an actual bounded test choose and state \(N>0\), a specified family inside
\(H_N\), and any regularity bound used for finite generation checks. This
record does not supply an uncomputed value of \(N\) or an actual supported
boundary ideal. Positive-characteristic comparisons are separate cases with
their characteristic stated.

## Question

For a specified nonempty family of moving ideals in \(H_N\), which points
have their whole saturated homogeneous ideal generated by two sextics? Can
one construct such a point, or exclude it on that entire declared family?
When using opposite limits, the family must realize the proposed pair of
limits simultaneously, not through two unrelated one-sided deformations.

## Purpose

[F-0001](../../frameworks/torus_supported_boundaries.md) preserves support and
Hilbert data at a full torus boundary while testing equations on moving ideals.
This investigation turns that strategy into a bounded realization problem.
Its success can be a construction or an informative family-specific exclusion;
neither outcome alone classifies all supported intersections.

## Inputs

[R-0037](../../results/supported_hilbert_full_limits.md) gives the bounded
incidence and the generic length nine forced by this polynomial.
[R-0039](../../results/moving_ideal_generator_criterion.md) is the exact
generation criterion. [R-0046](../../results/sextic_three_generator_full_limit.md)
is a residual control outside \(H_N\), despite having the same polynomial.
Its degree-ten generator warns against testing only the sextic piece.

## Method

First specify actual ideals or an actual flat family, verifying
\(\mathcal I_C^N\subset\mathcal J\subset\mathcal I_C\), the polynomial,
and any endpoint or embedded structure. Compute both full torus limits when
this helps describe the family. Such limits need not be two-generated.

On strata with correct graded fibers, calculate multiplication maps
\(S_1\otimes J_{d-1}\to J_d\). The target is
\(\dim M(J)_6=2\) and \(M(J)_d=0\) otherwise. A finite range requires
a justified uniform bound \(\operatorname{reg}_S J\le r\), \(r\ge6\),
on this family. Two independent sextic sections, a tangent vector, or an
abstract Gorenstein algebra is insufficient. A proposed equation pair must
generate the entire ideal scheme-theoretically.

An arbitrary supported deformation to the desired CI is useful even if it
does not realize a prescribed torus orbit history. Keep that construction
problem distinct from the stronger prescribed-boundary problem.

## Findings

This is an unperformed mathematical investigation. The pilot has reconstructed
the criteria and controls, not produced a supported invariant boundary of this
polynomial, an attracting-family classification, or an exhaustive incidence
calculation. The residual three-generator example is not such a boundary.
There is no inferred nonemptiness or universal sextic exclusion.

## Completion and next decisions

Complete one declared family by an actual supported pair, a proof of emptiness
of its full two-equation incidence, or a precisely identified missing lifting
or generator condition. A local failure can redirect that family search.
An all-pairs conclusion still needs coverage of the relevant fixed limits and
power bounds. An unbounded-degree conclusion needs a further argument.
Keep any exploration and useful data in this directory when work starts;
catalog independently reusable findings as Results and preserve complete proofs.
