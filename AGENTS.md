# STCI agent retrieval protocol

Read [INDEX](research/INDEX.md) first and use [MAP](research/MAP.md) for
mathematical orientation before dated notes. Find the smallest relevant
statement, proof, or question and read its actual dependencies.

Identify the exact curve, characteristic, hypotheses, and question. Study
containment and residual components before imposing sole support. A vertical
exceptional component can arise from special-point geometry. For torus limits,
keep the full ideal, Hilbert polynomial, support, embedded and endpoint
structure, and additional generators. Distinguish the full ideal from two
initial forms, normal-cone ideals, and cycles. On bounded supported loci, use
opposite limits and attracting families where useful and test actual generation
on the moving ideal. Fixed limits may require more generators. Any coverage
claim needs its own proof and explicit degree, power, regularity, and
characteristic bounds.

Use the [research model](research/MODEL.md) for object formats and the
[evidence and review rules](research/MODEL.md#evidence-and-review) for assessing
claims and reporting substantial work. Follow the
[conflict rules](research/MODEL.md#corrections-and-conflicting-claims) when
claims disagree and the
[exact proof support and retention rules](research/MODEL.md#exact-proof-support-and-retention)
before retiring files.

Coordinate INDEX, MAP, STATE, FRONTIER, and the decision guide when reading
routes or research direction change. Preserve informative negative findings and
independent constructive approaches. Use the decision guide to compare options.
Follow the [validation commands and stacked-PR integration rules](research/MODEL.md#commands-and-integration).
