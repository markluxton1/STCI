# STCI research

For the temporary corpus cleanup, consult the [canonical structural inventory](research/CORPUS_TREE_INVENTORY.md) and [topic proposals](research/CORPUS_STRUCTURAL_PROPOSALS.md). These are not mathematical proof verification.

Start at the [lane routes](research/INDEX.md#find-a-statement-proof-or-open-question)
and [framework map](research/MAP.md). Use `python3 scripts/research.py frameworks`
to discover approaches, `show ID` for proofs and declared consumers,
and `route LANE --compact` or `section ADDRESS` for scoped reading.
Read the owning proof and necessary evidence before rederiving a claim.

Record the exact curve, characteristic, hypotheses and question. Study
containment and residual support before imposing sole support. For a torus
limit track the full saturated ideal, Hilbert polynomial, endpoint and
embedded data, and generators. Test generation on the moving ideal and
justify coverage of any exclusion.

Use the [research workflow](research/MODEL.md#research-roles-and-delegation)
and [contribution workflow](research/MODEL.md#contribution-workflow).
Choose a bounded question with explicit inputs and stopping condition; delegate
only when useful, with narrow scopes and minimal inherited context. Investigators
report evidence, limitations and implications to their parent. Independently
review consequential claims before accepting them; integrate reviewed mathematics
into its owner and check plausible impacts on parallel Frameworks, not just declared
consumers. Retire temporary agents and investigation notes after integration.
Run relevant [checks](research/MODEL.md#commands-and-integration).
